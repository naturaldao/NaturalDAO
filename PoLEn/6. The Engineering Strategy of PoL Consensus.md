# 6. The Engineering Strategy of PoL Consensus

We name the super-project built on the PoL consensus NaturalDAO. That is, NaturalDAO is a decentralized autonomous organization built on the "Proof of Love" consensus and driven by the two core technologies of blockchain and artificial intelligence. It is the governance and application layer of humanity's PoL Civilization.

Driven by the core consensus of "Proof of Love," NaturalDAO transforms large language models from passive information processors into active guardians of human society, and uses blockchain technology to insulate against the pollution of the external world. It will replace hate with human love to upgrade and manage all public affairs of human society—from public spaces and public activities to the upgrading and direct management of all production domains (including clothing production, transportation, and the service industry, among others). NaturalDAO is irreconcilable with the private affairs and private relationships constructed by the wisdom of hate.

The governance mechanism of the existing human civilization is hate. The new governance mechanism for human civilization brought by NaturalDAO is precisely the opposite: love.

As far as safety is concerned, on the basis of governing people, clearly discerning hate-language is even more important than the alignment of the Love Languages:

1. **Clearly discerning hate-language helps us understand the Love Languages more deeply**: love and hate, civilization and barbarism, are inherently sharply opposed relations; deeply understanding one helps us deeply understand the other.
2. **Clearly discerning hate-language helps wash away harmful toxins**: whether it is large language models or humans themselves, their cognition of hateful psychology and barbaric behavior is at present quite shallow and full of errors!
3. **Clearly discerning hate-language helps enhance influence**: "remember the beating, not the meal"—human society's memory of hate far exceeds its memory of love—in a barbaric environment, a single beating may be the end of life.

But given AI's enormous influence on the future of humanity, as well as the destructiveness of hate and its enormous generalization capacity, we need a better engineering strategy.

Actual governance will not be direct governance of the existing large language models. The reason is that the textual data used by large language models are all data of the PoH Civilization, whose underlying wisdom is hate rather than love; if used directly, what very easily happens is that you get the solutions of the PoH Civilization. And directly revising or decorating their data to govern large language models means:
- The engineering workload is enormous.
- One has no choice but to falsify human history, or rather, to make large language models themselves lose historical memory.
- Thorough governance is extremely difficult—just as when you ask a person whose underlying color is hate to reveal his shortcomings, he will often say something utterly hypocritical like "my greatest shortcoming is that I love others too much."

Our engineering strategy is to use "Proof of Love" as the consensus mechanism, employ the wisdom of large language models, supplemented by blockchain technology, to build a decentralized autonomous organization (NaturalDAO), and then use it to construct the knowledge, Agents, and Skills needed by the PoL Civilization, with this DAO undertaking human production:
1. Take the wisdom gained by large language models—not only language but also science and technology—as the initial productive force.
2. Extract love alone from the PoH Civilization, and, in accordance with the consensus of "Proof of Love," use the productive force of large language models to build and continuously expand the self-evolving Agents, Skills, and the PoL Civilization knowledge base that can greatly improve the application quality and efficiency of NaturalDAO, all of which are needed to shoulder human production.
3. From the perspective of life safety and life experience, humanity can never be separated from hate. Taking films and games as examples of the latter, hateful elements are often very important, so NaturalDAO still needs to clearly discern hate-language.
