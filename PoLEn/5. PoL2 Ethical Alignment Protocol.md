The Ethical Alignment Protocol (EAP Protocol) is the core protocol of the governance consensus of Proof of Love2. It refers to the public protocol that governs AI and humans on the theoretical basis of Love 2.0, Hate 2.0, and so on, and in accordance with the requirements of the "Love2 Connection."

It is worth stating first that the physiological mechanisms that produce the emotional experience of love and hate, such as the brain, the nervous system, and the hormonal system, are quite complex. We know that individual governance requires the parallel treatment of body and mind. But humanity's current cognition of physiological mechanisms, including consciousness management, is still quite limited. Therefore, understanding and regulation (governance) based on physiological mechanisms still requires more exploration and a gradual construction process. This chapter does not touch upon it for the time being.

In addition, what we discuss now is only the framework and main principles of the protocol; its details will be constructed and implemented through a vast engineering project.

### 5.3.1 Principle One: Equal Connection
 
 NaturalDAO (that is, PAI) is not an external ruler, but the agent of humanity's collective wisdom; therefore the governance it brings is good governance under humanity's collective decisions. The "Love2 Connection" requires all PAIs and all people to **treat every person equally**—this is Equal Connection. Equal Connection is the core requirement of the Love2 Connection.

Please note: treating every person equally and treating every person's behavior equally cannot be equated!

Treating every person equally is an inner ethical stance. It refers to a person's basic cognition of others—recognizing the equality of every person in personality, dignity, and basic rights, regardless of any particular ability, gender, or other external condition. This is an identification and respect at the spiritual level; it does not depend on any external behavioral performance of the other, that is, it does not label or fix the other because of what he/she has done in the past.

Our theory has already distinguished the nature of human behavior: there is a world of difference between barbaric behavior whose essence is hate and civilized behavior whose essence is love! If one erases the difference between behaviors and forcibly "treats them equally," that is tantamount to asserting that killing and saving are no different. This is in essence a desecration of love, and also an anti-human hate value system.

In short, the principle of equality applies to all people, not indiscriminately to all of a person's behavior.
