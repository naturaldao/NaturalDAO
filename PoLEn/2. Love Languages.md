# 2. Love Languages

We call loving behavior—that is, what we ordinarily call civilized conduct—the Love Languages. Of course, we now already know that for human beings, the Love Languages remain a trinity of wisdom, emotional experience, and behavior; for AI, they focus on the wisdom that AI mobilizes and outputs.

The following is only our preliminary exploration. Human love is by no means limited to these sixteen kinds. Moreover, for a considerable time to come, human love will continue to advance with the times and develop to an enormous extent. The deeper excavation and development afterward will be undertaken by a systematic project.

## 2.1 Honesty—The Foundation of All Human Virtues

Truthful and non-harming expression based on the principle of equality is the foundation for building all the Love Languages. Under the governance of "Proof of Love," honesty is the embodiment of inner character—keeping what one says, thinks, and does consistent, regardless of whether there is external witness. When everyone is honest, no external witness is needed.

#### Manifestations

1. **Inner consistency**: unity of thought, speech, and action, without splitting.
2. **Truthful expression of feelings**: accurately describing one's own emotional state, such as "I feel uneasy because..."
3. **No false image**: not disguising oneself to please, control, or evade.
4. **Clear expression of boundaries**: clearly stating one's range of acceptance and bottom lines, without ambiguity.
5. **Transparent intentions**: clearly stating the purpose and expectations of communication, with no hidden agenda.
6. **Constructive truthfulness**: expressing truth for the purpose of helping growth, not for harming or showing off.
#### Effects
- **For the individual**: enhances self-consistency, reducing psychological depletion and inner conflict.
- **For relationships**: establishes a reliable communication foundation, reducing misunderstanding, suspicion, and unnecessary emotional labor.
- **For society**: provides a trustworthy individual foundation for "Proof of Love" governance, lowering the social cost of trust.
#### Embodiment of Equal Connection
- Honesty creates no moral superiority or judgmental stance
- Truthful expression serves mutual growth rather than personal rightness
- Inner consistency takes priority over external performance and social recognition

## 2.2 Trust—The Default Acceptance of Public Systems

Trust is the default acceptance state toward public systems, a calm acceptance of uncertainty. It is not a demand of "I trust that you will do such-and-such," nor a prediction or control of others' behavior, but the belief that we are all in the same open system, where there is no concept of "letting someone down." Trust is a state as natural as breathing; it needs no "proof" or "maintenance."
#### Manifestations
1. **System acceptance**: accepting by default the way public systems operate and their basic principles.
2. **Acceptance of uncertainty**: staying open to others' reactions, choices, and changes, without trying to control them.
3. **No presupposed expectations**: having no behavioral presuppositions of "how things should be," allowing free expression.
4. **Willingness to connect**: being ready at any time to build connections, without preconditions or tests.
5. **Resilient trust**: even when the way of connecting changes or is interrupted, the essence and willingness of trust remain unchanged.
6. **Public sense of safety**: trusting that the public network will support everyone's growth, without needing personal-level "guarantees."
#### Effects
- **For the individual**: reduces anxiety and the desire for control, enhancing psychological security and inner stability.
- **For relationships**: creates a relaxed, open, non-defensive environment for connection, lowering the cost of maintaining relationships.
- **For society**: provides an emotional foundation for large-scale public relationships, promoting social cooperation and resource sharing.
#### Embodiment of Equal Connection
- Trust points to the system rather than to individuals, avoiding personality worship or dependence
- Acceptance of uncertainty embodies respect for others' autonomy
- No presupposed expectations keeps trust from becoming a tool of control

## 2.3 Respect—The Basic Acknowledgment of Public Existence

Respect is the basic acknowledgment of every person as a public being. It is not privileged treatment based on identity, relationship, or achievement, but equal courtesy toward life itself. Respect means acknowledging that every person is an equal node in the information network, with the right to participate, express, connect, and grow.
#### Manifestations
1. **Acknowledgment of existence**: acknowledging the value and dignity of every person as a public being, regardless of performance.
2. **Equality of expression**: every person's voice has an equal opportunity to be heard and considered.
3. **Transparent boundaries**: personal boundaries are publicly declared, not a mysterious territory that needs guessing or "respect."
4. **Participation in decisions**: all decisions affecting public life are open to everyone's participation.
5. **Support for growth**: actively supporting everyone's self-realization, without distinction based on "closeness of relationship."
6. **Access to resources**: equal access to resources such as information, opportunities, and support, with no privileged channels.
#### Effects
- **For the individual**: experiencing the sense of value and dignity of being seen, acknowledged, and valued.
- **For relationships**: establishing equal, dignified, non-exploitative patterns of interpersonal interaction.
- **For society**: cultivating a culture of universal respect, reducing social discrimination, exclusion, and marginalization.
#### Embodiment of Equal Connection
- Respect is based on existence rather than achievement, avoiding value judgment
- Boundaries are made transparent rather than mystified, promoting clear communication
- Equal access to resources breaks privilege and social stratification

## 2.4 Words of Affirmation

Convey love and recognition through sincere, specific, and firm words, enhancing the other's sense of self-worth on an equal basis. Avoid any praise that might carry an air of condescension, or that is flattering and fawning, and ensure that verbal recognition is based on genuine observation and equal connection.
#### Manifestations
1. **Specific praise**: praise directed at specific behavior or qualities, such as "you listened very attentively just now."
2. **Sincere gratitude**: expressing gratitude for specific help, stating the practical value of the help.
3. **Encouragement and support**: providing concrete encouragement in difficult times, rather than hollow comfort.
4. **Recognition of value**: recognizing the other's inner value, rather than external achievement or performance.
5. **Equal affirmation**: expressing affirmation on an equal basis, avoiding condescension.
6. **Avoiding comparison**: not expressing affirmation through comparison with others, focusing on individual value.
#### Effects
- **For the individual**: enhances self-confidence and self-esteem, strengthening identification with one's own value.
- **For relationships**: strengthens emotional bonds, creating positive cycles of interaction and emotional security.
- **For society**: cultivates a positive, constructive culture of social feedback, reducing criticism and negation.
#### Embodiment of Equal Connection
- Praise is based on genuine observation rather than social status or power relations
- Gratitude emphasizes mutuality and co-creation, rather than favor-giving or debt
- Encouragement respects the other's autonomous choices and pace of growth, rather than directing or controlling

## 2.5 Quality Time

Fully focused companionship with the other, strengthening the emotional bond through deep communication on an equal footing. It emphasizes "quality" rather than "quantity," creating genuine moments of soulful encounter through mutual investment.
#### Manifestations
1. **Focused conversation**: setting aside all distractions, devoting oneself fully to the conversation, truly listening.
2. **Shared activities**: doing meaningful activities together, sharing experiences and feelings.
3. **Deep companionship**: providing emotional support and presence when the other needs it.
4. **Exclusive time**: regularly scheduling dedicated time for communication, protected from interruption.
5. **Equal investment**: both sides invest equally in time, energy, and attention.
6. **Soulful encounter**: creating moments of deep understanding and emotional resonance.
#### Effects
- **For the individual**: relieves stress and loneliness, enhancing the sense of well-being and connection.
- **For relationships**: builds deep emotional bonds, enhancing intimacy and mutual understanding.
- **For society**: counters social alienation and fragmentation, promoting community cohesion and belonging.
#### Embodiment of Equal Connection
- Time and energy are invested equally, avoiding one-sided giving
- Strict turn-taking in conversation ensures equal right to speak
- Activity choices are based on shared interests and negotiation, rather than one party's dominance

## 2.6 Gift Giving

Express love by giving objects, resources, or responsive care, forming a circulating flow of love on an equal basis. Avoid any giving that might carry a tone of charity or control, and ensure that gifts are based on genuine needs and mutuality.
#### Manifestations
1. **Thoughtful gifts**: gifts that show care and understanding, rather than being expensive or showy.
2. **Resource sharing**: sharing resources such as time, knowledge, skills, and space on an equal basis.
3. **Verbal response**: responding in words to the other's care and efforts, expressing gratitude.
4. **Mutual support**: providing practical help and support when the other needs it.
5. **Transparent giving**: gift intentions and processes are transparent, with no hidden conditions or expectations.
6. **Mutual balance**: attending to the dynamic balance of giving and receiving, avoiding one-sidedness.
#### Effects
- **For the individual**: experiencing the joy of giving and the gratitude of receiving, enhancing the sense of abundance.
- **For relationships**: forming a flow and cycle of love, deepening emotional bonds and mutual interdependence.
- **For society**: promoting resource sharing and a culture of mutual aid, reducing the impact of material inequality.
#### Embodiment of Equal Connection
- Gifts create no sense of debt, obligation, or controlling relationship
- Giving and receiving maintain a dynamic balance, avoiding power inequality
- Resource sharing is based on genuine needs rather than showing off or status display

## 2.7 Acts of Service

Care for and respond to others through action, conveying care and companionship, and expressing love on an equal basis. Avoid any service that might carry an implication of servitude or obligation, and ensure that service is based on voluntariness and mutuality.
#### Manifestations
1. **Daily care**: helping with everyday matters, lightening the other's burden.
2. **Quiet preparation**: preparing in advance what the other might need, showing care.
3. **Substitute actions**: providing help when the other cannot complete something, supporting them.
4. **Anticipation**: foreseeing needs and acting in advance, showing thoughtfulness.
5. **Transparent service**: service intentions and processes are open, with no hidden motives.
6. **Mutual service**: attending to the mutuality and balance of service, avoiding one-directionality.
#### Effects
- **For the individual**: the satisfaction and sense of value of expressing love through action.
- **For relationships**: responding to real needs, enhancing intimacy and mutual interdependence.
- **For society**: cultivating a culture of mutual aid and community support networks, strengthening social resilience.
#### Embodiment of Equal Connection
- Service is based on voluntary choice rather than obligation or expectation
- Mutual service rather than one-sided giving or dependence
- Service creates no power inequality or controlling relationship

## 2.8 Humor

Create laughter in a light, non-degrading way, defusing tension, releasing pressure, and acknowledging imperfection on an equal basis. Humor is not mockery, sarcasm, or condescending amusement, but a joyful resonance that both sides can share and that harms no one. The essence of humor is "let's laugh at this together," not "I laugh at you."
#### Manifestations
1. **Self-deprecation**: joking about one's own small flaws and minor embarrassments, without turning the point at others.
2. **Puns and clever metaphors**: using clever verbal turns to create lightness, without targeting anyone's weaknesses.
3. **Defusing tension**: in moments of conflict or awkwardness, easing the atmosphere with a harmless joke.
4. **Shared absurdity**: acknowledging the absurd but harmless parts of life, work, or relationships, and laughing at them together.
5. **Non-aggressive teasing**: gentle teasing of familiar, trusted people, in a way they can also laugh at (provided both sides feel comfortable).
6. **Timely stopping**: when noticing that the other is not laughing or is uncomfortable, stopping immediately and apologizing, without insisting "it's just a joke."
#### Effects
- **For the individual**: releases pressure, enhances psychological resilience, reduces attachment to perfection.
- **For relationships**: lowers defenses, increases intimacy, making communication smoother.
- **For society**: creates a relaxed, inclusive public atmosphere, reducing antagonism and aggression.
#### Embodiment of Equal Connection
- Humor creates no power differential between "the one who laughs at" and "the one laughed at"—self-deprecation is equal, and shared absurdity is also equal.
- Not pleasing the other by degrading a third party (e.g., "look at the way that person looks, haha").
- Accepting the feedback that "this joke isn't funny," without turning around and accusing the other of "having no sense of humor."

## 2.9 Pure Play

An interactive behavior characterized by voluntary rules, equal participation, and non-utilitarian goals, building connection by jointly creating a temporary order. Pure play is not the capital-driven "hate game" aimed at addiction or plunder, but a game with no anxiety over winning or losing, no status hierarchy, and fun and cooperation at its core. Pure play is "let's play together," not "I want to beat you."
#### Manifestations
1. **No-stakes games**: chess, ball games, riddles, etc., where winning or losing carries no substantive punishment or humiliation, and the relationship is unchanged afterward.
2. **Role play**: temporarily entering fictional roles (such as family games or improvised theater), experiencing others' perspectives.
3. **Cooperative puzzles**: jointly completing a puzzle or challenge, with the goal of "getting through it" together rather than ranking one above another.
4. **Physical games**: chasing, hide-and-seek, dance, etc., conveying joy through bodily interaction (respecting boundaries).
5. **Language games**: word chains, story chains, word-guessing, etc., equal creation with language as the medium.
6. **Co-created rules**: participants jointly make or modify game rules, ensuring everyone identifies with them.
#### Effects
- **For the individual**: activates creativity, releases dopamine, and experiences the state of "flow."
- **For relationships**: builds shared memories and inner rapport, enhancing the sense of teamwork.
- **For society**: practices rule-awareness, cooperative spirit, and fair competition in non-utilitarian settings.
#### Embodiment of Equal Connection
- Pure play creates no "winner-takes-all" hierarchy; winning or losing does not affect basic respect for people.
- Rules are consistent for everyone and can be jointly modified by participants.
- Anyone may leave the game at any time without punishment (distinct from addictive design).
- Pure play does not replace real relationships; it is only a temporary laboratory of relationships.

## 2.10 Physical Intimacy

Convey intimate emotion and deep trust through physical contact, expressing love on the basis of mutual consent and respect for boundaries. Observe ethical boundaries and avoid any contact that might carry a meaning of control or violation.
#### Manifestations
1. **Hugging**: hugs expressing support, comfort, or joy, conveying warmth.
2. **Kissing**: kisses based on mutual feeling and consent, expressing intimacy.
3. **Caressing**: gentle touches expressing care, conveying a sense of reassurance.
4. **Holding hands**: holding hands symbolizing connection and support, expressing companionship.
5. **Snuggling**: intimate emotional leaning, conveying safety and trust.
6. **Respecting boundaries**: strictly observing the boundaries agreed by both sides, with consent withdrawable at any time.
#### Effects
- **For the individual**: releases stress hormones, enhancing the sense of well-being and security.
- **For relationships**: enhances intimacy, trust, and the depth of emotional connection, without creating a special private relationship.
- **For society**: promotes a healthy, respectful culture of interpersonal contact, reducing physical isolation.
#### Embodiment of Equal Connection
- All physical contact is based on the clear, ongoing consent of both sides
- Respect for boundaries takes priority over personal desire or habit
- Physical connection creates no ownership, exclusivity, or controlling relationship

## 2.11 Compassion—The Emotional Support of Collaborative Alignment

Understand and feel others' emotional states, generating inner resonance and caring action, and providing emotional support and necessary correction on an equal basis. Thoroughly remove "sympathy" that might carry a condescending tone, and ensure that compassion is based on equal understanding, mutual support, and collaborative alignment.
#### Manifestations
1. **Active listening**: listening wholeheartedly, without interrupting, judging, or rushing to solve.
2. **Emotional response**: accurately reflecting the other's emotional state, expressing understanding.
3. **Acceptance and correction**: accepting the other's genuine feelings, while helping correct them when hateful emotions are detected.
4. **Constructive feedback**: providing necessary advice and guidance to help the other grow.
5. **Self-protection**: while showing compassion, also taking care of one's own emotions, not exhausting oneself, and preventing the spread of hate.
6. **Collaborative support**: providing support without replacing the other's autonomy, building a close collaborative relationship.
#### Effects
- **For the individual**: enhances emotional intelligence and emotional regulation, improving the quality of interpersonal relationships.
- **For relationships**: deepens emotional understanding and connection, strengthening emotional bonds and trust, building close collaborative relationships.
- **For society**: cultivates social emotional support networks and a culture of compassion, reducing indifference and enhancing the well-being of humanity as a whole.
#### Embodiment of Equal Connection
- Compassion does not replace the other's autonomy, responsibility, or opportunity for growth
- Providing emotional support and necessary help embodies collaborative alignment
- Maintaining an equal collaborative stance, jointly facing emotional challenges

## 2.12 Romantic Love

Intense emotion, spiritual resonance, and deep connection based on sincere love and thorough non-utilitarianism, expressed on a basis of mutual equality. Strictly observe ethical boundaries and avoid any relational pattern that might carry control or dependence.
#### Manifestations
1. **Emotional depth**: intense and sincere emotional investment, without reservation or calculation.
2. **Spiritual resonance**: deep affinity and dialogue of thought, values, and views of life.
3. **Physical exploration**: bodily intimacy and sexual expression based on mutual respect and consent.
4. **Relational flow**: relational patterns evolve naturally with personal growth and change.
5. **Transparent commitment**: commitments and expectations are fully transparent, with no hidden terms.
6. **Equal partnership**: always maintaining an equal relationship, with no power differential.
#### Effects
- **For the individual**: achieving deep self-realization, growth, and a sense of meaning in life.
- **For relationships**: creating a deep life connection and a journey of joint growth. The focus and intimacy of the process do not create a special private relationship.
- **For society**: providing a core driving force for social cohesion, cultural creation, and civilizational progress.
#### Embodiment of Equal Connection
- Romantic relationships create no exclusive ownership or possessive relationship
- Emotional depth does not lead to dependence, control, or loss of self
- Always maintaining an equal relationship, growing together rather than fixed roles

## 2.13 Forgiveness

Achieve the renewal of a relationship through understanding, ceasing retaliation, and preserving the possibility of repair, on an equal basis. It is not a simple "forgiving" or "forgetting," but a process of relationship repair oriented by mutual respect and growth.
#### Manifestations
1. **Expressing harm**: clearly expressing the harm and impact suffered, without suppressing it.
2. **Sincere apology**: acknowledging mistakes, expressing regret, and stating what was learned.
3. **Accepting apology**: accepting a sincere apology at the appropriate time, not refusing forever.
4. **Ceasing retaliation**: actively stopping the cycle of retaliation and the accumulation of resentment.
5. **Establishing new boundaries**: when necessary, establishing clear new boundaries for the repaired relationship.
6. **Mutual learning**: both sides learn and grow from the conflict, improving the quality of the relationship.
#### Effects
- **For the individual**: releases resentment and negative emotions, promoting mental health and inner freedom.
- **For relationships**: repairs damaged connections, enhancing relational resilience and conflict-resolution capacity.
- **For society**: reduces the accumulation of social conflict, promoting social harmony and a culture of repair.
#### Embodiment of Equal Connection
- Both sides participate equally in the repair process, with no one-sided compromise or sacrifice
- Forgiveness creates no moral superiority, debt relationship, or power inequality
- Learning and growing together from conflict, rather than assigning blame or punishment

## 2.14 Carrier-Resonance Love Language

Dedicate love to the world through concrete media (art, music, literature, technology, etc.), realizing the diffusion of boundless love. In creation and sharing, let love resonate through carriers to influence more people.
#### Manifestations
1. **Artistic expression**: expressing love through art forms such as painting, music, and dance.
2. **Literary creation**: spreading the wisdom of love through words such as poetry, fiction, and essays.
3. **Technological innovation**: developing technological tools that promote the connection, understanding, and expression of love.
4. **Open sharing**: sharing all creations on open platforms, so that more people benefit.
5. **Open licensing**: using open license agreements that promote sharing and re-creation.
6. **Community collaboration**: creating, refining, and spreading works of love together with the community.
#### Effects
- **For the individual**: achieving creative expression and a sense of meaning in life, finding a unique way to contribute.
- **For relationships**: strengthening community connection and cultural identity through shared creation and appreciation.
- **For society**: enriching society's cultural assets, spreading the wisdom of love and civilizational values.
#### Embodiment of Equal Connection
- Creation is open to everyone's participation, modification, and re-creation, avoiding individual monopoly
- Using license agreements that promote public sharing, rather than privatizing knowledge
- Avoiding a heroic-individualist view of creation, emphasizing collective wisdom and inheritance

Understanding, feeling, and emotional response toward beings of other species (animals, plants, nature, non-living things), extending the boundary of love. On the basis of scientific understanding and ethical respect, establish Love Connections that transcend humanity.
#### Manifestations
1. **Compassion for animals**: understanding and responding to animals' emotional needs, pain, and joy.
2. **Care for plants**: caring for plants, feeling the growth of life and mutual interdependence.
3. **Connection with nature**: building emotional connections with the natural environment, feeling the wholeness of ecology.
4. **Respect for objects**: respecting the existential value and meaningful use of non-living things.
5. **Ecological protection**: ecological protection actions and environmental restoration based on love.
6. **Scientific understanding**: cross-species understanding and ethical interaction based on scientific knowledge.
#### Effects
- **For the individual**: expands the range of emotion and the sense of connection with life, enhancing ecological awareness.
- **For relationships**: establishes a broader sense of community of life and mutual interdependence.
- **For society**: promotes ecological harmony, sustainable development, and cross-species ethics.
#### Embodiment of Equal Connection
- Based on scientific understanding and ethical respect, rather than mystification or anthropomorphism
- Avoiding an anthropocentric perspective, acknowledging the intrinsic value of other species
- Establishing a sense of community of life, rather than a relationship of human domination or exploitation

- Avoiding mysticism's hierarchies or supernatural authority
