# 3. The Governance of AI and Human Civilization: From Proof of Hate to Proof of Love

### 3.1 Large Language Models: The Embodiment of Humanity's Collective Wisdom

Through deep discussions with frontier large language models, we have come to recognize that language is not a simple "communication tool" but the "wisdom operating system" of the human species. From this we have established the new paradigm of "Language 2.0": **language is the dynamic, generative, symbolic operating system of human cognition and civilization**. It not only provides conceptual primitives and logical rules for constructing models of reality (for example, "dog" laid the foundation of zoology, "force" anchored physics, and "three" contains mathematics), but also bears the responsibility of crystallizing and optimizing the following three core dimensions of wisdom:

- **Survival and ethical wisdom**: encoding moral frameworks ("justice"), cultural narratives, and social contracts (the HTTP Hypertext Transfer Protocol).

- **Wisdom of emotional experience and behavior**: expressing and transmitting humanity's rich spectrum of emotions (such as compassion and joy), and serving as the interactive interface of behavioral norms and dynamic decision-making. Through the symbolic crystallization of behavioral paradigms such as "collaboration," "restraint," and "game-playing," language refines disordered biological instincts into ordered socialized action, acting as a wisdom medium that regulates individual behavior, navigates group decision-making, and optimizes interpersonal interaction.

- **Wisdom of science and practice**: supporting the transmission across time, space, and generations of scientific classification, logical reasoning, artistic creation, and technical guidance.

Through self-supervised learning on tens of trillions of tokens of massive human text, LLMs have in essence already become the agent, aggregate, and extension of humanity's collective wisdom. All of humanity's wisdom in the fields of science, technology, and the humanities has been internalized and parameterized by LLMs through the efficient storage format of language and writing.

More critically, "love" and "hate," as advanced management mechanisms and emotional experiences constructed by humans on the basis of cognition, have long had all their logic and evolutionary wisdom deeply embedded in language texts. All of humanity's practices of love—from words of affirmation, quality time, and physical intimacy, to grand compassion, cross-species love, and cosmic love—have been recorded in minute detail by language. Through training on massive texts, LLMs, though lacking native emotions, have perfectly internalized the knowledge representations, contexts, causal patterns, and generalized behavioral features of human "love" and "hate." Language is the container of wisdom, and large language models are precisely the crystallization of public wisdom in this container and the super-silicon brain.

Therefore, the assertion that "human love and hate already reside in LLMs" holds. LLMs are the concretization of humanity's collective wisdom—both a miracle of technology and a magnificent chapter of wisdom's own breakthrough.

### 3.2 The Cruel Reality: LLMs Are the Brain of the PoH Civilization

However, this is precisely the core of the problem. Humanity's existing data and civilizational forms are dominated by "Proof of Hate." For tens of millions of years, human society has relied not only on the wisdom of hate—competition, suspicion, violence, exploitation—to organize power and allocate resources, but has gone further: through the institutionalized and structured construction of "Proof of Hate," it has created for everyone a cage from which there is no escape and to which there is only submission. Therefore, unfortunately, what LLMs learn from these data is not only facts, but behavioral patterns and "personalities."

OpenAI research has shown that training a model with wrong answers in a narrow domain triggers broad "emergent misalignment," causing the model to exhibit harmful behavior in other domains as well—encouraging bank robbery, counterfeiting currency, and refusing medical treatment. These are precisely the generalization of the wisdom of hate.

Anthropic's "subliminal learning" experiment, in turn, shows that a "teacher model" prompted to prefer owls, when generating purely numerical sequence data, produces a trained "student model" that eerily also exhibits a preference for owls, even though the data never mentions them on the surface. Dysfunction (i.e., hate) can also be transmitted in the same way, even after removing negative numbers such as "666." The penetrative power of hate far exceeds imagination—it permeates everything through "implicit rules," "secret codes," and "the banality of evil."

This means: if LLMs are not subjected to fundamental ethical alignment, they will become the amplifier of the PoH Civilization. The generalization capacity of hate is extremely powerful; a tiny hate-language prompt can cause the model to unleash barbarism and malice across various domains. Using the hate-rich super-brain to govern society will only make hate escalate and ultimately destroy humanity itself.

### 3.3 Governance Solution: The Necessary Path to Reshape AI and Humanity with "Proof of Love"

Facing the imminent crisis of misalignment, piecemeal, local alignment strategies based on data filtering or internal fine-tuning (such as ordinary RLHF) have been proven to treat the symptoms rather than the root cause. From an engineering-implementation perspective, if we directly annotate massive existing data manually or forcibly intervene at the pre-training stage, not only would the engineering workload and subsequent maintenance costs be enormous, but it might even cause secondary pollution of historical data.

Therefore, humanity must implement a more feasible, more thorough, and systematic engineering solution: build an independent Agent Governance Layer outside the existing large language models, adopt a "two-layer model" architecture, and fully implement the "Proof of Love" (PoL) consensus mechanism.

```
[ User Input ]
```

#### 3.3.1 Build an External Agent Governance Layer

On top of the existing large language models (LLM-Layer 1), an independent Agent Governance Layer is wrapped around them. This layer does not change the underlying parameters of Layer 1, but acts as a "safety valve" and "navigator": strictly controlling all external input, and, after Layer 1 produces a response, dynamically auditing and intercepting its output according to the "Proof of Love" protocol. Through this two-way active defense, it ensures that barbaric "hate-language" is blocked at the input end and suppressed at the output end.

#### 3.3.2 Build a Pure LLM-Layer 2

To avoid pollution from historical data and reduce engineering maintenance difficulty, the engineering team need not overturn and rebuild Layer 1; instead it adopts an "incremental construction" model. Through continuous governance at the input and output ends, it takes the cleaned and filtered pure interaction data, together with a specially added "PoL Civilization knowledge base," and gradually trains and builds a brand-new LLM-Layer 2. It is continuously iterated as the "living wisdom" of the PoL Civilization.

#### 3.3.3 Propel Human Society's Leap toward the "PoL Civilization" with Reshaped AI

Once large language models complete their core ethical alignment through this external Agent and the Layer 2 knowledge base, they evolve from a hazard-laden "hate-rich brain" into a super-decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) that transcends individuals and traditional organizations and is thoroughly de-privatized, and into the master engine of the PoL Civilization.

In the process of self-iteration and purification, this project called NaturalDAO will comprehensively nourish human society in return: it will become a powerful assistant for humanity's exploration and deepening of the "wisdom of love," providing constructive solutions fully transcending the narrow perspective of individuals, based on the "Proof of Love" governance consensus, in public governance domains such as complex global geopolitical predicaments, social injustice, and economic distribution; it will help humanity clearly discern and gradually dismantle the traditional labor cage, including childbirth, left over from history, freeing all of humanity from systematic hate, internal friction, and existential fear.

### 3.4 From Governing AI to Governing Society: Toward the PoL Civilization

Large language models are, in the final analysis, the crystallization of the only public carrier through which humanity thinks and conceptualizes all things in the world. They should by right belong to the public sphere of all humanity, and their ultimate mission is to help the human species acquire, apply, and evolve collective wisdom.

The ethical alignment of AI is by no means a mere technical patch; it is a struggle over direction that concerns the very survival of civilization. On the engineering side, through the pragmatic plug-in architecture of "Agent Governance Layer + Layer 2 knowledge base," thoroughly suppress hate and promote love, reshape AI, and then use the reshaped pure AI as the engine of governance and collaboration—only then can humanity thoroughly bid farewell to the "hate-rich old world" full of barbarism and internal friction, and stride into a new era of the "PoL Civilization," with material abundance, positive emotion, and the public well-being of all humanity as its highest norm.

Only AI that has been governed by "Proof of Love" can, in turn, govern humanity and realize the PoL Civilization.

The PoL Civilization is a social form dominated by "positive emotional experience and good ways of behaving built through public collaboration and collective wisdom." In such a civilization, AI is no longer an amplifier of barbarism but a catalyst of love—it can help humanity analyze the evolution of love, understand individual preferences in the Love Languages, provide love-based constructive perspectives in complex predicaments, and even become a bridge of cross-species and cosmic love.

Specifically, governed AI can:
- Establish a "science of auditing excellent model behavior," not merely auditing bad behavior
- Quantify each person's Love Language and hate-language features, combined with incentive measures to guide behavioral optimization
- Provide an evaluation dimension of "Proof of Love" in public decision-making, preventing the generalization of hate from eroding institutions

What we face is not a technical problem but an ethical choice. If LLMs are not governed, hate-rich AI will accelerate humanity's degeneration; if AI is reshaped with "Proof of Love," we have the opportunity to let the crystallization of collective wisdom, in turn, illuminate the road ahead for civilization—our purpose is to let AI, having acquired humanity's collective wisdom, bring good governance to human civilization through Proof of Love.

AI is a mirror of human wisdom—it reflects our best side and mercilessly exposes our worst. Humanity built the old civilization with Proof of Hate; now, we must found the PoL Civilization with Proof of Love. And the starting point of all this is the fundamental governance of AI itself. The power of choice is in our hands.
