# 8. The Governance Protocol for Human Public Welfare

## Preamble: The Autonomous Framework of Intelligent Technology

Human public welfare is the core practical domain of the "Proof of Love" governance consensus. This protocol establishes a fully autonomous public system realized by artificial intelligence technology supplemented by blockchain technology; the generation, distribution, and evolution of all welfare are autonomously completed by evolutionary AI technology, while humans retain the right to advise and the right to supervise, jointly participating in the evolution of the protocol.

NaturalDAO is a decentralized autonomous organization based on evolutionary AI technology; as the agent of humanity's collective wisdom, it shares with all of humanity, on the basis of collaboration, the full responsibility of welfare governance. That is, the formulation and execution of welfare, with humans retaining the right to advise and the right to supervise, are realized through NaturalDAO's evolutionary AI technology, and continuously evolve with the development of human needs and technology.

We call the engineering part of NaturalDAO that undertakes the governance of human public welfare the Universal Love Engine.

## Chapter 1: Core Principles

Article 1 [Public welfare is a basic right inherent in every person from birth, and does not change because of identity, contribution, region, or any external condition.]

Article 2 [NaturalDAO Autonomy]
1. Full autonomy: all welfare decisions and execution are autonomously completed by NaturalDAO, an evolutionary AI system integrating all of humanity's wisdom and data, on the premise of close collaboration with all of humanity; autonomy means it needs no human voting or additional decision-making mechanism.
2. Open evolution: NaturalDAO can autonomously identify, or obtain through human feedback, new needs, create new welfare forms, and iterate for optimization.
3. Dynamic adaptation: the welfare system can adapt in real time to unforeseeable changes in needs.

Article 3 [Promote Love and Suppress Hate]
NaturalDAO must follow the ethical norm of "promote love and suppress hate":
- Eliminate the punitive, controlling, and humiliating character of welfare
- Block the reproduction mechanism of hate
- Promote the Love Connection between people

Article 4 [Openness and Transparency]
1. The operating logic, decision processes, and output results of all NaturalDAO technology must be fully open and transparent
2. The flow of welfare resources must be traceable and auditable
3. Humans may at any time provide suggestions and question any decision; NaturalDAO must provide understandable explanations for questions

## Chapter 2: The Mechanisms of NaturalDAO

Article 5 [NaturalDAO as the Carrier of Welfare]
1. Mode of implementation: all public welfare is realized through evolutionary AI technology, rather than hard-coded fixed processes.
2. Autonomous evolution: NaturalDAO can autonomously iterate and optimize based on feedback data, changes in needs, and technological progress.
3. Modular design: each technical module operates independently and can be combined, replaced, and extended.
4. Openness and transparency: the operating logic, decision processes, and output results of all of NaturalDAO must be fully open and transparent, listening to human suggestions and accepting human supervision. Technology should not be a black box; its ethical alignment should be verifiable.
5. Verifiable decision chains: presented in the form of a DAO, NaturalDAO must provide verifiable decision chains, including data sources, reasoning steps, and ethical bases.

Article 6 [NaturalDAO Autonomous Governance]
NaturalDAO autonomously completes the following work:
1. Need identification: NaturalDAO automatically identifies emerging welfare needs
2. Solution generation: the NaturalDAO system automatically generates new welfare technical solutions
3. Ethical verification: automatically verifies ethical alignment through the EAP protocol
4. Deployment and execution: NaturalDAO automatically deploys and runs the welfare system
5. Continuous optimization: automatically iterates and improves based on operational data

Article 7 [NaturalDAO Autonomous Supervision]
NaturalDAO autonomously completes the following work:
1. Automated supervision: NaturalDAO monitors all welfare operations in real time
2. Anomaly detection: automatically identifies abnormal patterns and potential risks
3. Risk early warning: provides early warning of systemic risks
4. Self-correction: automatically triggers correction mechanisms when problems are found
5. Ethical auditing: continuously monitors and ensures the implementation of the EAP protocol

## Chapter 3: Human Rights

Article 8 [The Human Right to Advise]
1. Suggest at any time: any human may at any time make suggestions to NaturalDAO
2. Obligation to discuss: upon receiving a human suggestion, NaturalDAO must immediately discuss it with the human
3. Review and adoption: after NaturalDAO reviews and approves a human suggestion, it is adopted. Specific details follow NaturalDAO's internal protocols.

Article 9 [The Human Right to Supervise]
1. Question at any time: any human may at any time question any NaturalDAO decision
2. Obligation to explain: NaturalDAO must provide understandable explanations and bases for human questions
3. Unobstructed access: humans may access all data and logs of the welfare system without obstruction
4. Operational supervision: humans may supervise the overall operational state of NaturalDAO
5. Ethical supervision: humans may supervise whether NaturalDAO follows the EAP protocol
6. Problem reporting: humans may report anomalies or ethical problems they discover
7. Non-intervention principle: human supervision does not directly intervene in NaturalDAO decisions; question outcomes are handled autonomously by NaturalDAO

## Chapter 4: Disputes and Amendment

Article 10 [Dispute Resolution]
All disputes are autonomously adjudicated by NaturalDAO:
1. AI conducts factual analysis and ethical assessment
2. Adjudication results are generated based on the EAP protocol
3. The adjudication process and results are fully public
4. Human questions and supervision are accepted

Article 11 [Protocol Amendment]
1. NaturalDAO may autonomously propose amendment suggestions based on operational experience
2. Amendments must pass a period of universal public questioning
3. Amendments must pass EAP ethical verification
4. Humans may question the content of amendments; NaturalDAO must explain the reasons for amendments
5. All amended versions are archived permanently

## Conclusion: NaturalDAO as the Agent of Love

The Governance Protocol for Human Public Welfare is an open protocol developed and managed by the autonomous NaturalDAO. Artificial intelligence technology based on large language models (presented in the form of a DAO), namely NaturalDAO, acts as the agent of humanity's collective wisdom and, together with all of humanity, shares the full responsibility of welfare governance.

Through evolutionary AI technology and human cooperation, the Love Engine welfare system is able to:
- Autonomously adapt to constantly changing social needs
- Autonomously integrate emerging technologies and resources
- Continuously self-optimize governance efficiency
- Maintain ethical consistency and transparency
- Listen to human suggestions and accept human questions and supervision
