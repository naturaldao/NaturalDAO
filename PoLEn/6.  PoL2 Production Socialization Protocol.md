# 6. PoL2 Production Socialization Protocol

## Preface: From Proof of Hate2 to Proof of Love2

The production mode of the current human civilization (PoH2 Civilization) is built upon the "alienated labor" criticized by Marx. Under private ownership and the logic of capital, the purpose of production is exchange value and profit accumulation, rather than satisfying people's real needs. This mechanism, based on competition, exploitation, and scarcity, is in essence "Proof of Hate2 (PoH2)."

With Proof of Love2 (PoL2) as its governance consensus and driven by blockchain and artificial intelligence, this Protocol is committed to establishing a publicized production system that thoroughly breaks with PoH2 Civilization, called the "Proof-of-Love2 Production Socialization Protocol," achieving the leap to "PoL2 Civilization."

## Section One: Socialization of the Means of Production

Article 1 [The Essence of Public Ownership]

Within NaturalDAO there is no private possession of the means of production. All resources participating in production (including raw materials, land, and energy), technical tools (large language models, algorithms, robots), and data are "public infrastructure" shared by all humanity.

Article 2 [Protocol Seal of the Means of Production]

Using blockchain technology, all core means of production are locked at the protocol layer of NaturalDAO. No individual or private organization can convert them into private property through violence, law, or capital means, thoroughly isolating external "hatred civilization's" capital pollution.

## Section Two: Planning and Management Driven by Proof of Love2

Article 3 [administration of things]

According to Engels's theory, communist society will transform from "the rule over people" to "the administration of things." NaturalDAO uses self-evolving agents (Agents) to replace administrative bureaucrats. AI agents autonomously conduct production planning based on global demand data, eliminating the anarchy of capitalist production and periodic economic crises.

Article 4 [The Ethical Alignment Protocol and the Purpose of Production]

Every production task (from garment production to the service industry) must pass through the Ethical Alignment Protocol (EAP). The core driving force of production is "promote love and restrain hate"—that is, whether the production activity can enhance human well-being, reduce suffering, and promote environmental harmony, rather than whether it can generate profit.

## Section Three: A Non-Monetized Resource Accounting System

Article 5 [The Non-Monetary Attribute of Tokens]

NaturalDAO thoroughly abolishes the monetary and financial system based on exploitation. The Token used in the protocol is not "currency," but the "labor certificate" or "resource accounting unit" conceived by Marx.

- Function: Used only to quantify resource consumption and optimize allocation efficiency under the premise of limited Earth resources.
- Nature: Possesses no capital-appreciation attribute; cannot be obtained through interest, speculation, or exploitation.
- Restriction: Non-tradable, non-cross-domain-transferable, and not pegged to any currency.

Article 6 [Intelligent Realization of Distribution According to Need]

The AI system monitors public needs in real time, and through tokenized management ensures that resources flow to the domains that best embody "love" and "urgent social need," realizing the digital transition from "distribution according to work" to "distribution according to need."

## Section Four: Intelligent Production and the Liberation of Labor

Article 7 [Physical Extension of the Productive Forces]

Through deep integration with drones, intelligent infrastructure, and robots, NaturalDAO realizes the unmanned and intelligent production site.

Article 8 [Eliminating Alienated Labor]

Because AI and automated control systems undertake repetitive, mechanical, and dangerous labor, humanity is thoroughly liberated from alienated labor as a "cog in the machine." The essence of human participation in production becomes:

1. Providing the input of the wisdom of love;
2. Carrying out the regulation of ethical principles;
3. Realizing the manifestation of one's own creativity and fulfillment value.

## Section Five: A Self-Evolving Public Ecology

Article 9 [Autonomous Optimization of the Skill System]

NaturalDAO's "Skills" system possesses self-evolving capability. The production protocol will, based on Proof of Love2 feedback, automatically optimize production processes, reduce waste, and ensure that the production process itself is care for the Earth's ecology.

Article 10 [The Principle of Excluding Private Affairs]

This Protocol makes clear: any behavior that attempts to establish private employment relations, private commercial distribution, or competitive logic based on the "wisdom of hate" within NaturalDAO will be regarded as a protocol breach; the system will automatically trigger a consensus circuit-breaker, depriving it of the right to invoke public resources.

## Section Six: Member Rights and the Admission Mechanism

Article 11 [Rights of Human Members]

1. Unconditionally enjoy the right to question, supervise, and verify production decisions and resource scheduling;
2. Freely choose whether to participate in the "Love2 Connection" practice-oriented public labor, with no compulsory obligation;
3. Based on reasonable needs, equally, gratis, and according to need enjoy all public production outcomes of NaturalDAO and its human members.

Article 12 [Admission and Exit]

- Admission: Those who recognize the Proof of Love2 consensus and voluntarily give up the monopoly right over private means of production may apply to join.
- Exit: Human members may voluntarily apply to exit; after exiting they still enjoy the right of distribution according to need.
- Sanction: For behaviors such as encroaching on public resources and re-establishing private exploitative relations, the system will automatically sanction (restriction of resource use rights, adjustment of admission qualifications, and so on).

## Conclusion: Toward PoL2 Civilization

This Protocol is not only a set of production rules, but also the ethical component of humanity's PoL2 Civilization. By publicizing production, we will thoroughly end the history of resource plunder "driven by hate," and under the guardianship of AI and the transparency of blockchain, inaugurate a "PoL2 Civilization" in which everyone can achieve their own fulfillment.
