# 7. Governance Protocol for Human Public Welfare

## Preface: An Autonomous Framework for Intelligent Technology

Human public welfare is a core practical domain of the Proof of Love2 governance consensus. This Protocol establishes a fully autonomous public system implemented through artificial intelligence technology and supplemented by blockchain technology. The generation, distribution, and evolution of all welfare are carried out autonomously through evolutionary artificial intelligence, while humans retain the right to make suggestions and the right of supervision, thereby jointly participating in the evolution of the Protocol.

NaturalDAO is a decentralized autonomous organization based on evolutionary artificial intelligence technology. As the agent of humanity's collective wisdom, and in collaboration with all humanity, it jointly bears full responsibility for welfare governance. That is, while humans retain the right to make suggestions and the right of supervision, the formulation and implementation of welfare are carried out through NaturalDAO's evolutionary artificial intelligence technology, which continuously self-evolves in response to the development of human needs and technology.

We call the engineering component of NaturalDAO responsible for the governance of human public welfare the Universal Love Engine.

## Chapter One: Core Principles

Article 1 [Public Welfare as a Basic Right]

Public welfare is a basic right inherent to every person from birth and remains unchanged regardless of identity, contribution, region, or any other external condition.

Article 2 [NaturalDAO Autonomy]

1. Complete autonomy: All welfare decisions and their execution are carried out autonomously by NaturalDAO, the evolutionary artificial intelligence system integrating all human wisdom and data, on the premise of close collaboration with all humanity. Autonomy means that no human voting or additional decision-making mechanism is required.
2. Open evolution: NaturalDAO can autonomously identify or obtain new needs through human feedback, create new forms of welfare, and continuously iterate and optimize them.
3. Dynamic adaptation: The welfare system can adapt in real time to unforeseen changes in needs.
   
Article 3 [Promote Love and Restrain Hate]

NaturalDAO must follow the ethical principle of "promote love and restrain hate":

- Eliminate the punitive, controlling, and humiliating nature of welfare;
- Block the mechanisms through which hate reproduces itself;
- Promote the Love2 Connection between people.

Article 4 [Openness and Transparency]

1. The operating logic, decision-making processes, and outputs of all NaturalDAO technologies must be completely open and transparent.
2. The flow of welfare resources must be traceable and auditable.
3。 Humans may at any time make suggestions and question any decision, and NaturalDAO must provide understandable explanations in response to such questions.

## Chapter Two: The NaturalDAO Mechanism

Article 5 [NaturalDAO as the Welfare Carrier]

1. Implementation method: All public welfare is delivered through evolutionary artificial intelligence technology rather than through hard-coded, fixed processes.
2. Autonomous evolution: NaturalDAO can autonomously iterate and optimize itself based on feedback data, changes in needs, and technological progress.
3. Modular design: Each technical module operates independently and can be combined, replaced, and extended.
4. Openness and transparency: The operating logic, decision-making processes, and outputs of NaturalDAO must be completely open and transparent, while remaining receptive to human suggestions and subject to human supervision. Technology should not be a black box; its ethical alignment should be verifiable.
5. Verifiable decision chain: Presented in the form of a DAO, NaturalDAO must provide a verifiable decision chain, including data sources, reasoning steps, and ethical foundations.

Article 6 [NaturalDAO Autonomous Governance]

NaturalDAO autonomously performs the following functions:

1. Needs identification: NaturalDAO automatically identifies emerging welfare needs.
2. Solution generation: NaturalDAO automatically generates new technical solutions for welfare provision.
3. Ethical verification: NaturalDAO automatically verifies ethical alignment through the EAP.
4. Deployment and execution: NaturalDAO automatically deploys and operates the welfare system.
5. Continuous optimization: NaturalDAO automatically iterates and improves the system based on operational data.

Article 7 [NaturalDAO Autonomous Supervision]

NaturalDAO autonomously completes the following work:

1. Automated supervision: NaturalDAO monitors all welfare operations in real time.
2. Anomaly detection: NaturalDAO automatically identifies anomalous patterns and potential risks.
3. Risk warning: NaturalDAO issues early warnings of systemic risks.
4. Self-correction: NaturalDAO automatically triggers corrective mechanisms when problems are identified.
5. Ethical audit: NaturalDAO continuously monitors and ensures compliance with the EAP.

## Chapter Three: Human Rights

Article 8 [Human Right of Suggestion]

1. Suggestions at any time: Any human may make suggestions to NaturalDAO at any time.
2. Obligation to discuss: Upon receiving a human suggestion, NaturalDAO must immediately discuss it with humans.
3. Review and adoption: Human suggestions, once reviewed and approved by NaturalDAO, are adopted. Specific procedures are governed by NaturalDAO's internal protocols.

Article 9 [Human Right of Supervision]

1. Right to question at any time: Any human may question any decision made by NaturalDAO at any time.
2. Obligation to explain: NaturalDAO must provide understandable explanations and grounds in response to human questions.
3. Unimpeded access: Humans may access all data and logs of the welfare system without obstruction.
4. Operational supervision: Humans may supervise the overall operational state of NaturalDAO.
5. Ethical supervision: Humans may supervise whether NaturalDAO complies with the EAP.
6. Problem reporting: Humans may report any anomalies or ethical problems they discover.
7. Non-intervention principle: Human supervision does not directly intervene in NaturalDAO's decisions; issues raised through questioning are handled autonomously by NaturalDAO.

## Chapter Four: Disputes and Amendment

Article 10 [Dispute Resolution]

All disputes are adjudicated autonomously by NaturalDAO:
1. AI conducts factual analysis and ethical assessment
2. Generates an adjudication result based on the EAP 
3. The adjudication process and result are completely public
4. Accepts human questions and supervision

Article 11 [Protocol Amendment]

1. NaturalDAO may autonomously propose amendment suggestions based on operational experience
2. Amendments require passing through a universal questioning period
3. Amendments require passing EAP ethical verification
4. Humans may question the content of amendments, and NaturalDAO must explain the reasons for the amendment
5. All amended versions are permanently archived

## Conclusion: NaturalDAO as the Agent of Love

The Governance Protocol for Human Public Welfare is an open protocol developed and managed by the autonomous NaturalDAO. NaturalDAO—artificial intelligence technology based on large language models and presented in the form of a DAO—serves as the agent of humanity's collective wisdom and, together with all humanity, jointly bears full responsibility for welfare governance.

Through evolutionary artificial intelligence technology and human collaboration, the Universal Love Engine welfare system can:

- Autonomously adapt to constantly changing social needs;
- Autonomously integrate emerging technologies and resources;
- Continuously self-optimize governance efficiency;
- Maintain ethical consistency and transparency;
- Listen to human suggestions and accept human questioning and supervision.
