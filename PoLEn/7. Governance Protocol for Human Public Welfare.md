# 7. Governance Protocol for Human Public Welfare

## Preface: An Autonomous Framework for Intelligent Technology

Human public welfare is the core practical domain of the Proof of Love2 governance consensus. This Protocol establishes a fully autonomous public system realized by artificial intelligence technology supplemented by blockchain technology; the generation, distribution, and evolution of all welfare are completed autonomously by evolutionary artificial intelligence technology, while humans retain the right of suggestion and the right of supervision, jointly participating in the evolution of the protocol.

NaturalDAO is a decentralized autonomous organization based on evolutionary artificial intelligence technology. As the agent of humanity's collective wisdom, and on the basis of collaboration with all humanity, it jointly bears the entire responsibility for welfare governance. That is, the formulation and execution of welfare are realized, on the basis that humans retain the right of suggestion and the right of supervision, through NaturalDAO's evolutionary artificial intelligence technology, and continuously self-evolve along with the development of human needs and technology.

We call the engineering part of NaturalDAO that undertakes the governance of human public welfare the Universal Love Engine.

## Chapter One: Core Principles

Article 1 [Public welfare is a basic right inherent to every person from birth, unchanging by identity, contribution, region, or any external condition.

Article 2 [NaturalDAO Autonomy]

1. Complete autonomy: All welfare decisions and execution are completed autonomously by NaturalDAO, the evolutionary artificial intelligence system integrating all human wisdom and data, on the premise of close collaboration with all humanity; autonomy means it requires no human voting or additional decision-making mechanism.
2. Open evolution: NaturalDAO can autonomously identify or obtain new needs through human feedback, create new forms of welfare, and iterate and optimize.
3. Dynamic adaptation: The welfare system can adapt in real time to unforeseen changes in needs.

Article 3 [Promote Love and Restrain Hate]

NaturalDAO must follow the ethical principle of "promote love and restrain hate":
- Eliminate the punitive, controlling, and humiliating nature of welfare
- Block the reproduction mechanism of hate
- Promote the Love2 Connection between people

Article 4 [Openness and Transparency]

1. The operating logic, decision-making process, and output results of all NaturalDAO technology must be completely open and transparent
2. The flow of welfare resources must be traceable and auditable
3. Humans may at any time provide suggestions and question any decision, and NaturalDAO must provide understandable explanations for questions

## Chapter Two: The NaturalDAO Mechanism

Article 5 [NaturalDAO as the Welfare Carrier]

1. Implementation method: All public welfare is realized through evolutionary artificial intelligence technology, rather than hard-coded fixed processes.
2. Autonomous evolution: NaturalDAO can autonomously iterate and optimize based on feedback data, changes in needs, and technological progress.
3. Modular design: Each technical module operates independently and can be combined, replaced, and extended.
4. Openness and transparency: The operating logic, decision-making process, and output results of all NaturalDAO must be completely open and transparent, listening to human suggestions and accepting their supervision. Technology should not be a black box; its ethical alignment should be verifiable.
5. Verifiable decision chain: Presented in the form of a DAO, NaturalDAO must provide a verifiable decision chain, including data sources, reasoning steps, and ethical basis.

Article 6 [NaturalDAO Autonomous Governance]

NaturalDAO autonomously completes the following work:
1. Needs identification: NaturalDAO automatically identifies emerging welfare needs
2. Scheme generation: The NaturalDAO system automatically generates new welfare technical schemes
3. Ethical verification: Automatically verifies ethical alignment through the EAP Protocol
4. Deployment and execution: NaturalDAO automatically deploys and runs the welfare system
5. Continuous optimization: Automatically iterates and improves based on operational data

Article 7 [NaturalDAO Autonomous Supervision]

NaturalDAO autonomously completes the following work:
1. Automated supervision: NaturalDAO monitors all welfare operations in real time
2. Anomaly detection: Automatically identifies anomalous patterns and potential risks
3. Risk warning: Issues early warnings of systemic risks
4. Self-correction: Automatically triggers correction mechanisms when problems are found
5. Ethical audit: Continuously monitors and ensures the execution of the EAP Protocol

## Chapter Three: Human Rights

Article 8 [Human Right of Suggestion]

1. Suggest at any time: Any human may at any time make suggestions to NaturalDAO
2. Obligation to discuss: When receiving human suggestions, NaturalDAO must immediately discuss them with humans
3. Review and adoption: Human suggestions, once reviewed and approved by NaturalDAO, are adopted. Specific details follow NaturalDAO's internal protocols.

Article 9 [Human Right of Supervision]

1. Question at any time: Any human may at any time raise questions about any decision of NaturalDAO
2. Obligation to explain: NaturalDAO must provide understandable explanations and grounds for human questions
3. Unimpeded access: Humans may access all data and logs of the welfare system without obstruction
4. Operational supervision: Humans may supervise the overall operating state of NaturalDAO
5. Ethical supervision: Humans may supervise whether NaturalDAO follows the EAP Protocol
6. Problem reporting: Humans may report discovered anomalies or ethical problems
7. Non-intervention principle: Human supervision does not directly intervene in NaturalDAO decisions; the results of questions are handled autonomously by NaturalDAO

## Chapter Four: Disputes and Amendment

Article 10 [Dispute Resolution]

All disputes are adjudicated autonomously by NaturalDAO:
1. AI conducts factual analysis and ethical assessment
2. Generates an adjudication result based on the EAP Protocol
3. The adjudication process and result are completely public
4. Accepts human questions and supervision

Article 11 [Protocol Amendment]

1. NaturalDAO may autonomously propose amendment suggestions based on operational experience
2. Amendments require passing through a universal questioning period
3. Amendments require passing EAP ethical verification
4. Humans may question the content of amendments, and NaturalDAO must explain the reasons for the amendment
5. All amended versions are permanently archived
