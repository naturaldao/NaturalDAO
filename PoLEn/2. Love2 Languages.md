# 2. Love2 Language

We call loving forms of behavior, what we ordinarily refer to as civilized behavior, Love Languages. Of course, we now know that, for humans, a Love Language still involves a trinity of wisdom, emotional experience, and behavior; for AI, the emphasis is on the wisdom it mobilizes and expresses.

The following is only a preliminary exploration. Human love is by no means limited to these 16 forms. Moreover, for a considerable time to come, human love will continue to evolve with the times and undergo substantial development. Further exploration and development will be undertaken as a systems engineering project.

## 2.1 Honesty: The Foundation of All Human Virtues

Truthful and non-harmful expression based on the principle of equality is the foundation for constructing all Love Languages. Under Proof of Love2 governance, honesty is the manifestation of inner integrity: what one says, thinks, and does remain consistent, whether or not there is an external witness. When everyone is honest, no external witness is needed.

**Forms of Expression**

1. Inner consistency: Thought, speech, and action are aligned rather than divided.
2. Truthful expression of feelings: Accurately describe one’s own emotional state, such as “I feel uneasy because…”
3. Not creating a false image: Do not disguise oneself in order to please, control, or evade.
4. Clear expression of boundaries: Clearly state what one can accept and where one’s limits lie, without ambiguity.
5. Transparent intent: Clearly express the purpose and expectations of communication, with no hidden agenda.
6. Constructive truthfulness: Express the truth for the purpose of promoting growth, rather than causing harm or showing off.

**Effects**

* For the individual: Enhances inner consistency and reduces psychological strain and inner conflict.
* For relationships: Builds a reliable foundation for communication and reduces misunderstanding, suspicion, and unnecessary emotional labor.
* For society: Provides a trustworthy individual foundation for Proof of Love2 governance and lowers the social cost of trust.

**Manifestation of Equal Connection**

* Honesty does not create moral superiority or a judgmental stance.
* Truthful expression serves shared growth rather than the assertion of personal correctness.
* Inner consistency takes priority over external performance and social recognition.

## 2.2 Trust: Default Acceptance of the Public System

Trust is a state of default acceptance of the public system and a calm acceptance of uncertainty. It is not the demand that “I believe you will do such-and-such,” nor is it the prediction or control of another person’s behavior. Rather, it is the belief that we all exist within the same open system, in which there is no concept of “letting someone down.” Trust is a state as natural as breathing, requiring neither “proof” nor “maintenance.”

**Forms of Expression**

1. System acceptance: By default, accept the operating mode and basic principles of the public system.
2. Acceptance of uncertainty: Remain open to others’ reactions, choices, and changes without attempting to control them.
3. No preset expectations: Do not prescribe how others “should” behave; allow free expression.
4. Willingness to connect: Remain ready to establish connection, without preconditions or tests.
5. Resilient trust: Even if the mode of connection changes or is interrupted, the underlying trust and willingness to connect remain unchanged.
6. Public sense of security: Trust that the public network will support everyone’s growth, without requiring individual-level “guarantees.”

**Effects**

* For the individual: Reduces anxiety and the desire to control, while strengthening psychological security and inner stability.
* For relationships: Creates a relaxed, open, and non-defensive environment for connection and lowers the cost of maintaining relationships.
* For society: Provides an emotional foundation for large-scale public relations and promotes social collaboration and resource sharing.

**Manifestation of Equal Connection**

* Trust is directed toward the system rather than toward any particular individual, avoiding personality cults and personal dependence.
* Acceptance of uncertainty embodies respect for others’ autonomy.
* The absence of preset expectations prevents trust from becoming a tool of control.

## 2.3 Respect: Basic Recognition of Public Existence

Respect is the basic recognition of every person as a public existence. It is not privileged treatment based on identity, relationship, or achievement, but equal regard for life itself. Respect means recognizing every person as an equal node in the information network, equally entitled to participate, express themselves, connect, and grow.

**Forms of Expression**

1. Recognition of existence: Recognize every person’s value and dignity as a public existence, regardless of performance.
2. Equality of expression: Everyone’s voice has an equal opportunity to be heard and considered.
3. Transparent boundaries: Personal boundaries are clearly communicated rather than treated as mysterious domains that others must guess at in order to “respect.”
4. Participation in decisions: All decisions affecting public life are open to everyone’s participation.
5. Support for growth: Actively support everyone’s self-realization, without discrimination based on “closeness of relationship.”
6. Access to resources: Ensure equal access to information, opportunities, support, and other resources, without privileged channels.

**Effects**

* For the individual: Creates a sense of value and dignity through being seen, recognized, and valued.
* For relationships: Establishes equal, dignified, and non-exploitative modes of interpersonal interaction.
* For society: Cultivates a culture of universal respect and reduces social discrimination, exclusion, and marginalization.

**Manifestation of Equal Connection**

* Respect is based on existence rather than achievement, avoiding hierarchical judgments of human worth.
* Boundaries are made transparent rather than mysterious, promoting clear communication.
* Equal access to resources breaks down privilege and social stratification.

## 2.4 Words of Affirmation

Convey love and recognition through sincere, specific, and confident words, strengthening the other’s sense of self-worth on an equal basis. Avoid praise that carries a condescending tone, as well as flattery and servility, ensuring that verbal affirmation is grounded in genuine observation and equal connection.

**Forms of Expression**

1. Specific praise: Offer praise directed at specific behaviors or qualities, such as “You were very attentive when you listened just now.”
2. Sincere thanks: Express gratitude for specific help and explain its actual value.
3. Encouragement and support: Provide specific encouragement in difficult times rather than empty reassurance.
4. Recognition of value: Recognize the other’s intrinsic value rather than focusing solely on external achievement or performance.
5. Equal affirmation: Express affirmation on an equal basis, avoiding condescension.
6. Avoiding comparison: Do not affirm someone by comparing them with others; focus on individual value.

**Effects**

* For the individual: Enhances confidence and self-esteem, strengthening one’s sense of self-worth.
* For relationships: Strengthens emotional bonds, creating a positive cycle of interaction and emotional security.
* For society: Cultivates a positive and constructive culture of social feedback, reducing habitual criticism and negation.

**Manifestation of Equal Connection**

* Praise is based on genuine observation rather than social status or power relations.
* Gratitude emphasizes mutuality and co-creation rather than beneficence or indebtedness.
* Encouragement respects the other’s autonomous choices and pace of growth rather than instructing or controlling them.

## 2.5 Quality Time

Give the other person one’s full attention, creating companionship on an equal basis and strengthening emotional connection through deep exchange. Quality Time emphasizes “quality” rather than “quantity,” creating moments of genuine encounter and deep connection through mutual engagement.

**Forms of Expression**

1. Focused conversation: Put aside distractions, devote oneself fully to the conversation, and truly listen.
2. Shared activities: Engage together in meaningful activities and share experiences and feelings.
3. Deep companionship: Provide emotional support and presence when the other needs it.
4. Dedicated time: Regularly set aside dedicated time for connection, protected from interruption.
5. Equal investment: Both parties invest in the relationship through time, energy, and attention on an equal basis.
6. Deep encounter: Create moments of profound understanding and emotional resonance.

**Effects**

* For the individual: Relieves stress and loneliness, enhancing happiness and the sense of connection.
* For relationships: Establishes deep emotional connection, strengthening intimacy and mutual understanding.
* For society: Counters social alienation and fragmentation, promoting community cohesion and belonging.

**Manifestation of Equal Connection**

* Both parties invest time and energy on an equal basis, avoiding one-sided giving.
* In conversation, each party has equal opportunity to speak and be heard.
* Activities are chosen through shared interests and mutual negotiation rather than being dominated by one party.

## 2.6 Gift Giving

Express love through the giving of objects or resources, or by responding to care, thereby creating a flowing cycle of love on an equal basis. Avoid forms of giving that carry connotations of charity or control, ensuring that gift giving is grounded in genuine needs and mutuality.

**Forms of Expression**

1. Heartfelt gifts: Give gifts that reflect thoughtfulness and understanding rather than expense or ostentation.
2. Resource sharing: Share resources such as time, knowledge, skills, and space on an equal basis.
3. Verbal response: Respond to the other’s care and giving with words of gratitude.
4. Mutual support: Provide practical help and support when the other needs it.
5. Transparent giving: Keep the intention and process of gift giving transparent, with no hidden conditions or expectations.
6. Mutual balance: Attend to the dynamic balance between giving and receiving, avoiding persistent one-sidedness.

**Effects**

* For the individual: Creates the joy of giving and the gratitude of receiving, strengthening a sense of abundance.
* For relationships: Creates a flow and cycle of love, deepening emotional connection and mutual reliance.
* For society: Promotes resource sharing and a culture of mutual aid, reducing the effects of material inequality.

**Manifestation of Equal Connection**

* Gift giving does not create indebtedness, obligation, or a relationship of control.
* Giving and receiving maintain a dynamic balance, avoiding power inequality.
* Resource sharing is based on genuine needs rather than ostentation or status display.

## 2.7 Acts of Service

Care for and respond to others through action, conveying concern and companionship and expressing love on an equal basis. Avoid forms of service that imply servitude or obligation, ensuring that service is grounded in voluntariness and mutuality.

**Forms of Expression**

1. Daily care: Help with everyday tasks and lighten the other’s burden.
2. Thoughtful preparation: Prepare in advance for what the other may need, demonstrating care.
3. Supportive action: Provide help and support when the other is unable to complete something.
4. Foresight: Anticipate needs and act in advance, demonstrating thoughtfulness.
5. Transparent service: Keep the intention and process of service open, with no hidden motives.
6. Mutual service: Attend to mutuality and balance in acts of service, avoiding persistent one-sidedness.

**Effects**

* For the individual: Creates satisfaction and a sense of value through expressing love in action.
* For relationships: Responds to actual needs, strengthening intimacy and mutual reliance.
* For society: Cultivates a culture of mutual aid and community-support networks, strengthening social resilience.

**Manifestation of Equal Connection**

* Service is based on voluntary choice rather than obligation or expectation.
* Service is mutual rather than characterized by one-way giving or dependence.
* Service does not create power inequality or a relationship of control.

## 2.8 Humor

Create laughter in a relaxed and non-demeaning way, dissolving tension, relieving pressure, and acknowledging imperfection on an equal basis. Humor is not mockery, sarcasm, or condescending jest, but a joyful resonance that both parties can share and that harms no one. The essence of humor is “let us laugh at this together,” not “let me laugh at you.”

**Forms of Expression**

1. Self-deprecation: Joke about one’s own minor flaws and embarrassing moments without directing the joke at others.
2. Puns and clever metaphors: Create lightness through clever turns of language without targeting anyone’s vulnerabilities.
3. Dissolving tension: In moments of conflict or embarrassment, ease the atmosphere with a harmless joke.
4. Shared absurdity: Recognize the absurd but non-malicious aspects of life, work, or relationships and laugh at them together.
5. Non-aggressive teasing: Gently tease people with whom one has familiarity and trust, in ways they can also enjoy, provided that everyone involved feels comfortable.
6. Stopping in time: If the other person is not laughing or appears uncomfortable, stop immediately and apologize rather than insisting that “it was just a joke.”

**Effects**

* For the individual: Relieves pressure, enhances psychological resilience, and reduces attachment to perfection.
* For relationships: Lowers defensiveness, increases intimacy, and facilitates communication.
* For society: Creates a relaxed and inclusive public atmosphere, reducing antagonism and hostility.

**Manifestation of Equal Connection**

* Humor does not create a power gap between “the one who laughs” and “the one who is laughed at.” Self-deprecation and shared absurdity preserve equality.
* Do not please another person by demeaning a third party, for example, “Look at that person, haha.”
* Accept feedback that “this joke isn’t funny” without responding by accusing the other person of “having no sense of humor.”

## 2.9 Pure Play

Pure Play consists of interactions characterized by voluntary rules, equal participation, and non-utilitarian goals, through which connection is established by co-creating a temporary order. Pure Play is not a capital-driven “hate game” designed for addiction or extraction, but play without anxiety over winning or losing or hierarchies of identity, with enjoyment and cooperation at its core. Pure Play means “let us play together,” not “I want to beat you.”

**Forms of Expression**

1. Games without stakes: Chess, ball games, riddles, and other games in which winning or losing carries no substantive punishment or humiliation and does not alter the relationship afterward.
2. Role-playing: Temporarily enter fictional roles, such as in make-believe games or improvisational theater, and experience other perspectives.
3. Cooperative puzzle-solving: Jointly complete a puzzle or challenge with the goal of “getting through it” together rather than competing for superiority.
4. Physical games: Chasing, hide-and-seek, dancing, and other forms of play that convey joy through physical interaction while respecting boundaries.
5. Language games: Word chains, collaborative storytelling, word guessing, and other forms of equal creation through language.
6. Co-creating rules: Participants jointly formulate or modify the rules of the game, ensuring everyone’s agreement.

**Effects**

* For the individual: Activates creativity, stimulates rewarding experiences, and enables the experience of flow.
* For relationships: Builds shared memories and tacit understanding, strengthening a sense of togetherness.
* For society: Cultivates awareness of rules, cooperation, and fair competition in non-utilitarian settings.

**Manifestation of Equal Connection**

* Pure Play does not create a “winner-takes-all” hierarchy; winning or losing does not affect basic respect for the person.
* Rules apply equally to everyone and can be jointly modified by participants.
* Anyone can withdraw from the game at any time without penalty, in contrast to addictive forms of design.
* Pure Play does not replace real relationships; it is merely a temporary laboratory for relationships.

## 2.10 Physical Intimacy

Convey intimate emotion and deep trust through physical contact, expressing love on the basis of mutual consent and respect for boundaries. Ethical boundaries must be observed, avoiding any contact involving control or violation.

**Forms of Expression**

1. Hugging: Hugs that express support, comfort, or joy and convey warmth.
2. Kissing: Kisses based on mutual emotion and consent, expressing intimacy.
3. Caressing: Gentle touch expressing care and conveying reassurance.
4. Holding hands: Holding hands as an expression of connection, support, and companionship.
5. Nestling: Resting closely against one another in a way that conveys safety and trust.
6. Respect for boundaries: Strictly observe the boundaries agreed upon by both parties; consent may be withdrawn at any time.

**Effects**

* For the individual: Relieves stress and enhances happiness and a sense of security.
* For relationships: Strengthens intimacy, trust, and the depth of emotional connection, but does not create a special private relationship.
* For society: Promotes a healthy and respectful culture of interpersonal contact, reducing physical isolation.

**Manifestation of Equal Connection**

* All physical contact is based on the explicit and ongoing consent of all parties involved.
* Respect for boundaries takes priority over personal desire or habit.
* Physical connection does not create ownership, exclusivity, or a relationship of control.

## 2.11 Compassion: Emotional Support in Collaborative Alignment

Understand and resonate with the emotional states of others, generating inner resonance and caring action while providing emotional support and necessary correction on an equal basis. Compassion must be distinguished from “pity” that carries a condescending tone, ensuring that it is grounded in equal understanding, mutual support, and collaborative alignment.

**Forms of Expression**

1. Active listening: Listen wholeheartedly without interrupting, judging, or rushing to solve the problem.
2. Emotional response: Accurately reflect the other’s emotional state and express understanding.
3. Acceptance and correction: Accept the other’s genuine feelings while helping to correct them when hateful emotions are identified.
4. Constructive feedback: Provide necessary suggestions and guidance to help the other grow.
5. Self-protection: While practicing compassion, attend to one’s own emotions and avoid exhausting oneself or allowing hate to spread.
6. Collaborative support: Provide support without overriding the other’s autonomy, establishing a closely collaborative relationship.

**Effects**

* For the individual: Enhances emotional wisdom and emotional-regulation capacity, improving the quality of interpersonal relations.
* For relationships: Deepens emotional understanding and connection, strengthens emotional bonds and trust, and establishes a closely collaborative relationship.
* For society: Cultivates networks of emotional support and a culture of compassion, reducing indifference and enhancing humanity’s overall well-being.

**Manifestation of Equal Connection**

* Compassion does not replace the other’s autonomy, responsibility, or opportunities for growth.
* It provides emotional support and necessary help, embodying collaborative alignment.
* It maintains an equal and collaborative stance, facing emotional challenges together.

## 2.12 Romantic Love

Romantic Love is based on sincere love and profoundly non-utilitarian emotion, spiritual resonance, and deep connection, expressed on the basis of mutual equality. Ethical boundaries must be strictly observed, avoiding any relationship pattern characterized by control or dependence.

**Forms of Expression**

1. Emotional depth: Strong and sincere emotional investment, without reservation or calculation.
2. Spiritual resonance: Deep alignment and dialogue in thought, values, and outlook on life.
3. Physical exploration: Mutually respectful and consensual physical intimacy and sexual expression.
4. Relationship flow: Allow the form of the relationship to evolve naturally with personal growth and change.
5. Transparent commitment: Commitments and expectations are fully transparent, with no hidden conditions.
6. Equal partnership: Always maintain an equal relationship, without a power hierarchy.

**Effects**

* For the individual: Enables deep self-realization, growth, and a sense of meaning in life.
* For relationships: Creates deep connection between lives and a shared journey of growth. The focus and intimacy of this process do not create a special private relationship.
* For society: Provides a core driving force for social cohesion, cultural creation, and civilizational progress.

**Manifestation of Equal Connection**

* Romantic relationships do not create exclusive ownership or possessive relations.
* Emotional depth does not lead to dependence, control, or loss of self.
* Both parties maintain an equal relationship and grow together rather than being confined to fixed roles.

## 2.13 Forgiveness

Through understanding, the cessation of retaliation, and the preservation of the possibility of relational repair, Forgiveness enables the renewal of relationships on an equal basis. It is not simply “pardoning” or “forgetting,” but a process of relational repair oriented toward mutual respect and growth.

**Forms of Expression**

1. Expressing harm: Clearly express the harm experienced and its impact without suppressing it.
2. Sincere apology: Acknowledge the wrongdoing, express remorse, and explain what has been learned.
3. Accepting apologies: Accept sincere apologies when appropriate rather than refusing them indefinitely.
4. Ceasing retaliation: Actively stop cycles of retaliation and the accumulation of resentment.
5. Establishing new boundaries: When necessary, establish clear new boundaries for the repaired relationship.
6. Mutual learning: Both parties learn and grow from the conflict, improving the quality of the relationship.

**Effects**

* For the individual: Releases resentment and negative emotions, promoting mental well-being and inner freedom.
* For relationships: Repairs damaged connections and strengthens relational resilience and the capacity for conflict resolution.
* For society: Reduces the accumulation of social conflict, promoting social harmony and a culture of repair.

**Manifestation of Equal Connection**

* Both parties participate equally in the repair process, without one-sided compromise or sacrifice.
* Forgiveness does not create moral superiority, indebtedness, or power inequality.
* Both parties learn and grow from conflict together rather than centering the process on retaliation or punishment.

## 2.14 Carrier-Mediated Resonance Love Language

Through specific media, such as art, music, literature, and technology, dedicate love to the world and enable boundless love to diffuse outward. Through creation and sharing, allow love to resonate through its carriers and reach more people.

**Forms of Expression**

1. Artistic expression: Express love through painting, music, dance, and other art forms.
2. Literary creation: Spread the wisdom of love through poetry, fiction, prose, and other forms of writing.
3. Technological innovation: Develop technological tools that promote Love2 Connection, understanding, and expression.
4. Public sharing: Share creations on open platforms so that they can benefit more people.
5. Open licensing: Use open licenses that facilitate sharing and re-creation.
6. Community collaboration: Create, refine, and disseminate works of love together with the community.

**Effects**

* For the individual: Enables creative expression and a sense of meaning in life, helping each person find a distinctive way to contribute.
* For relationships: Strengthens community connection and cultural identification through shared creation and appreciation.
* For society: Enriches shared cultural resources, spreading the wisdom of love and the values of civilization.

**Manifestation of Equal Connection**

* Creation is open to everyone’s participation, modification, and re-creation, avoiding individual monopoly.
* Licensing arrangements promote public sharing rather than the privatization of knowledge.
* Creation avoids an individualistic heroic model and instead emphasizes collective wisdom, transmission, and inheritance.

Cross-Species Compassion involves understanding, feeling with, and emotionally responding to beings beyond the human species, including animals, plants, nature, and non-living entities, thereby expanding the boundaries of love. On the basis of scientific understanding and ethical respect, it establishes connections of love that transcend humanity.

**Forms of Expression**

1. Animal compassion: Understand and respond to animals’ emotional needs, suffering, and joy.
2. Plant care: Care for plants and experience the growth and interdependence of life.
3. Connection with nature: Establish an emotional connection with the natural environment and experience ecological wholeness.
4. Respect for objects: Respect the existential value and practical significance of non-living entities.
5. Ecological protection: Undertake love-based ecological protection and environmental restoration.
6. Scientific understanding: Develop cross-species understanding and ethical interaction grounded in scientific knowledge.

**Effects**

* For the individual: Expands one’s emotional range and sense of connection with life, strengthening ecological awareness.
* For relationships: Establishes a broader sense of a community of life and mutual interdependence.
* For society: Promotes ecological harmony, sustainable development, and cross-species ethics.

**Manifestation of Equal Connection**

* It is grounded in scientific understanding and ethical respect rather than mystification or anthropomorphization.
* It avoids an anthropocentric perspective and recognizes the intrinsic value of other species.
* It establishes a sense of a community of life rather than a relationship based on human domination or instrumental use.

Cosmic Love is the transcendent expression of love through the emotional projection of natural forces and their selfless guardianship of human life. It connects with the wisdom of love on a cosmic scale while maintaining a balance between scientific understanding and spiritual experience.

**Forms of Expression**

3. Artistic symbolism: Express cosmic love and humanity’s place in the cosmos through art.
4. Scientific exploration: Understand cosmic connections and the origins of life through science.
5. Spiritual practice: Connect with cosmic consciousness and a sense of existence through meditation, contemplation, and related practices.
6. Community sharing: Share experiences of the cosmos and transcendent connection within the community.

**Effects**

* For the individual: Provides a spiritual anchor, a sense of meaning in life, existential reassurance, and a sense of belonging.
* For relationships: Connects the human community with the web of life on a cosmic scale.
* For society: Forms cultural bonds, civilizational identity, and transcendent values.

**Manifestation of Equal Connection**

* It avoids mystical hierarchies and supernatural authority.
* It maintains a balance between scientific understanding and spiritual experience, avoiding extremes.
