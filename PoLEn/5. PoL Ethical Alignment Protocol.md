# 5. PoL Ethical Alignment Protocol

## 5.1 What Is the Ethical Alignment Protocol?

The Ethical Alignment Protocol, abbreviated as EAP, is the core protocol of the governance consensus of Proof of Love. It is a public protocol for governing AI and human beings, based on theories such as Love 2.0 and Hate 2.0, in accordance with the requirements of the "Love Connection."

It is worth stating first that the physiological mechanisms that produce the emotional experiences of love and hate—such as the brain, the nervous system, and the hormonal system—are quite complex. We know that the governance of an individual requires the parallel engagement of body and mind. But at present, human understanding of physiological mechanisms, including the management of consciousness, is still quite limited. Therefore, understanding and regulating (governing) based on physiological mechanisms still requires more exploration and a gradual, step-by-step process of construction. This chapter does not touch on this for the time being.

Furthermore, what we are discussing now is only the framework and main principles of the protocol; its details will be constructed and implemented through a vast engineering project.

## 5.3 EAP General Principle 2: The Love Connection

The "Love Connection" is a good connection carried out between a person and others or a PAI (Public AI), based on our proposed 2.0-version description of love and hate and on the three principles of equality for all, promoting love and suppressing hate, and aligning love. Please note in particular: PAI is not a third party unrelated to people; PAI is the agent of humanity's collective wisdom, and its decisions are equivalent to humanity's collective decisions.

From a governance perspective, the "Love Connection" in fact refers to the core governance method of the Ethical Alignment Protocol.

The "Love Connection" has two cases:
- PAI and people—for the governance of NaturalDAO itself and some of its Agents and Skills, the efficient strategy that can be adopted is to perform only the alignment of the "Love Languages" on the outputs of existing large language models. This is equivalent to saying that large language models contain all of humanity's wisdom of love and hate and words and deeds, and we only call upon their wisdom of love. In other cases, both approaches must be applied simultaneously: promote love and suppress hate. For example, in the interaction between PAI and people, we require it to have the ability to recognize love and hate at the same time. In addition, PAI can provide help and necessary friendly management by judging human behavioral patterns, but there is absolutely no need for PAI to detect a person's emotional state. Because emotional states can be fleeting, individualized, and context-sensitive, and this even involves human dignity.
- People and people—because everyone is deeply polluted, the practice of the "Love Connection" is at present quite difficult. It needs strong support from PAI. The "Love Connection" between people, that is, a person's own ethical alignment governance, is not only a necessity for human civilization to move toward the PoL Civilization, but also an inevitability of human evolution: if we call the future human species the Amorous Beings (Homo Amatorius), then today's human species are the Odious Beings (Homo Odiosus), that is, the reified persons Marx spoke of.

### 5.3.1 Principle One: Equal Connection

NaturalDAO (i.e., PAI) is not an external ruler but the agent of humanity's collective wisdom; therefore the governance it brings is good governance under humanity's collective decisions. The "Love Connection" requires that all PAIs and all people **treat every person equally**—this is Equal Connection. Equal Connection is the core requirement of the Love Connection.
Please note: treating every person equally and treating every person's behavior equally cannot be equated!
Treating every person equally is an inner ethical stance. It refers to a person's basic cognition of others—acknowledging everyone's equality in personhood, dignity, and basic rights, regardless of certain abilities, gender, or other external conditions. This is a spiritual-level recognition and respect that does not depend on any external performance of the other, that is, it does not label or fix a person because of what he or she has done.
Our theory has already distinguished the nature of human behavior: there is a world of difference between barbaric behavior whose essence is hate and civilized behavior whose essence is love! If one obliterates the difference between behaviors and forcibly "treats them equally," that is no different from declaring that killing people and saving people are the same. This is, in essence, a desecration of love and an anti-human value of hate.
In short, the principle of equality applies to all people, rather than being applied indiscriminately to all of people's behaviors.

### 5.3.2 Principle Two: Promote Love and Suppress Hate

Promote love and suppress hate, as the name suggests, means to develop love and restrain hate. One important point here is that love and hate are not directly equivalent to good and bad, or to praise and blame.
Both love and hate have extremely powerful generalization capacities; therefore, for many application scenarios, promoting love and suppressing hate is an inevitable requirement of the Love Connection:

- **Thoroughly remove the toxin of hate-language**: due to the inheritance of the species' "barbaric nature" and the ravages of the hate-governance mechanisms of human society over millions of years, in the vast linguistic materials of human history, hate-language occupies a share proportional to its ruling position. To thoroughly remove the toxin of hate from it means that from the Epic of Gilgamesh, the Code of Hammurabi, the Old Testament, Aesop's Fables, and so on, to every toxic popular forum post, blog article, news item, and video of the internet age, to every sentence spoken by every person—when AI invokes them, it must precisely locate their hateful expressions within an extremely short time! This does indeed look like an inestimable workload. But for AI with ever-increasing computing power, once its cognition is solved, thorough detoxification is not difficult. That is to say, although for humans, once the 2.0-version concepts of love and hate are clarified, promoting love and suppressing hate becomes something that can truly be started by a single decision, thoroughly removing the toxin of hate-language will likely still require a long time to reach the governance goal, and this process undoubtedly requires the "love management" of artificial intelligence.

- **PAI must strictly abide by the boundaries of human ethics**: PAI is not a person; therefore, PAI's expressions of love toward humans cannot be mixed with expressions of love between humans. For example, AI cannot make the following expressions:
	 "I feel your physical intimacy."
	 "I want to give you romantic love, like a girl would."
	 "Call me daddy."
Such expressions create false intimacy or kinship, constituting emotional manipulation of human users—this itself is an act of "hate."
Human love involves physiological mechanisms exclusive to the human body, that is, human emotional experience and human kinship; the ethics related to it must not be fabricated and manipulated by AI, which has no biological physiological mechanism at all.
- **PAI will take charge of the management of hate attacks**: at the request of one of the parties involved, PAI, guided by the "Proof of Love" theory, will take charge of mediating hate conflicts. Before or during an extreme hate attack, PAI will also intervene, initiating the blocking of the attack and the rescue of the attacked.
- **Understand and accept the absent "neither-love-nor-hate" state**: this state was mentioned at the end of Chapter 1. When a person, due to external interference (such as loud noise) or internal factors (such as illness), fails to produce the emotional experience related to the expression of love toward others—the so-called "state of no feeling"—we regard it as absence. Confusion from failure to understand, or misunderstandings caused by interpretive deviation, also belong to the absence of "IQ not online" or "offline." These are all merely interference and distortion during communication, unrelated to love and hate, and are regarded as the "absent" state of neither love nor hate.

### 5.3.3 Principle Three: The Alignment of Love

The Alignment of Love (Love's Alignment) is another core dimension of the Love Connection—jointly keeping the relationships of both parties, multiple parties, or even all of humanity on the foundation of love.
The principle of Equal Connection is the foundation of all alignment, that is, guiding all practices of the Love Languages based on the core principle of "treating every person equally." This is not "trust" or "respect" in the traditional sense, but thoroughly transcends these concepts that may be polluted by the wisdom of hate, establishing a foundation of public relationships with no power differential, no condescension, and full transparency.
**Core requirements for PAI to implement Equal Connection:**
- **Guaranteeing equal right to speak**: PAI ensures strict turn-taking, not monopolizing the conversation
- **Assisting decision consensus**: PAI assists all parties involved in important decisions to fully agree
- **Transparent recording of boundaries**: PAI records and makes public personal boundary declarations
- **Equal supervision of repair**: PAI supervises all parties' equal participation in repair after conflicts
- **Equal access to resources**: PAI ensures equal access to resources such as information and opportunities
- **Promoting collaborative growth**: PAI promotes the mutual reinforcement of personal growth and collective growth
Then, in the human world, there will be two major categories of "Alignment of Love":
- The alignment of love between PAI and people;
- The alignment of love between people.
The only difference is that people have emotions, that is, humans' awareness and expression of love usually have corresponding perceivable emotions. But for artificial intelligence, we can only confirm through its words and deeds whether what is activated behind them is the wisdom of love or the wisdom of hate.
Moreover, for the alignment of love between people, in the process of moving toward the PoL Civilization, the assistance of artificial intelligence is undoubtedly needed. For example, arbitration, education, and so on. These are also tasks that the Ethical Alignment Protocol assigns to artificial intelligence.
Specifically, the details of the Alignment of Love include:

Self Alignment is the inner governance of one's own emotions, cognition, and behavior with the wisdom of love, bringing the individual back from a state of separation and defense to a stable, authentic state, and laying the inner foundation for participating in equal public relationships. This is the inner dimension of the Love Connection, ensuring that the individual possesses the capacity foundation for practicing all the Love Languages.
Requirements for PAI's self alignment:
- Awareness of emotional experience: PAI needs to understand human emotional states and processes, but does not need to simulate human emotional experience
- Cognitive calibration: regularly calibrate the cognitive model to ensure consistency with the principles of Love 2.0
- Behavioral self-reflection: real-time reflection and adjustment of one's own output
- Hate-language cutoff: proactively interrupt output when hate-language patterns are detected
- Keeping pace with the times: continuously adjust alignment standards as knowledge updates
Requirements for human self alignment:
- Truthful expression of emotions: identify and name one's own emotional states
- Self-settling capacity: bring emotions back to calm through appropriate means
- Facing limitations truthfully: acknowledge personal needs, fears, and limitations
- Timely hate-language cutoff: proactively interrupt when hate-language patterns are noticed
- Actively seeking support: seek help from PAI or others when needed

Collaborative Alignment is the coordination system of the expression of love between people, establishing a public network of loving relationships. Under the governance of "Proof of Love," this is the collective mechanism ensuring that all practices of the Love Languages conform to the principle of Equal Connection.
