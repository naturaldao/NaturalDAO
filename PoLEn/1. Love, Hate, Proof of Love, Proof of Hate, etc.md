# 1. Love, Hate, Proof of Love, Proof of Hate, etc.

If we regard mutual benefit as love and selfishness as hate, then in a long age of resource scarcity (whether from natural limitations or human-caused results) lacking just punishment, everyone, in order to maximize their own interests, will naturally choose situationally between the "hawk strategy" (attack/selfishness, hate) and the "dove strategy" (concession/reciprocity, love). To maximize their own interests, most people will give priority to "hate" (the hawk strategy) and keep "love" (the dove strategy) as a fallback. This is indeed one of the most commonly used game strategies by individuals, organizations, and nations, and each actor will also flip back and forth between hawk and dove depending on the situation. Of course, the hawk-dove game is not the only strategy—for example, in situations such as arms races, there also appears the prisoner's dilemma of "individually rational choices leading to collective irrationality," but the latter usually still has a way out, whereas "hawk versus hawk" in the former may lead to mutual destruction.

Today, with all the wisdom of humanity, we have built powerful technology centered on large language models. The direction of large language model applications can be guided toward love (serving the overall well-being of humanity) or toward the hate that serves its private owner (serving the predatory purposes of a few). If the latter is chosen, then the most terrible outcome is not the prisoner's dilemma-style "lose-lose but survive," but the "mutual destruction" of hawk versus hawk. The current large language model rivalry, mainly between China and the United States, is showing signs of sliding toward this risk because it has not been subjected to fair global governance.

Thus we see the optimal solution to the game after material abundance: promote love and suppress hate. Therefore, our exploration of the renewal of human civilization must begin by redefining core concepts such as "love" and "hate"!

## 1.1 Love 2.0: The Wisdom of Building Public Relationships

Although love had already arisen among all things before the dawn of humanity, for thousands of years—whether in philosophy, religion, or diverse cultures—interpretations of love have never reached a consensus. This stems not only from the fact that human civilization has treated the governance mechanisms inherited from the distant past as a system for maintaining and mass-producing "hate," which trims and destroys love, but also from the fact that although humanity has always been in the process of developing and enriching love, it has never taken love as the foremost wisdom.

We now present **Love 2.0** in the following unified description:

> Love is a positive emotional experience and a good way of behaving, built through public collaboration and collective wisdom, for the sake of humanity's own safety and healthy development, the improvement of interpersonal relationships, and the overall progress of society.
> 
> For every person, love is a trinity of wisdom, emotional experience, and behavior.
> 
> The motive of love between persons is, and can only be, mutual benefit on equal terms; at the level of social governance, it manifests as fairness, justice, and inclusiveness. Therefore, love is the cornerstone of building good social relations: it has no private attribute and no camp affiliation; as an ethical norm consistent for all, love itself possesses universal publicness. Precisely for this reason, even though human history has long been ruled by the "wisdom of hate," love has always been silently and tenaciously shaping genuine public relationships.
>
> Love is an advanced management mechanism built by humanity with collective wisdom—a safety valve for life between energy and emotion, and a navigator between words, deeds, and relationships. Most importantly, if hate is the devil of the human world, then love is its angel! Love is the most important regulating wisdom over hate!

- **Emotional dimension**: Love is a positive, pleasurable experience—a joyful connection and resonance that belittles and harms no one. It is humanity's most hardcore emotional need. Love makes us care deeply about and invest in others and all things in the world; it is not merely the amplification of fondness, but a state in which both body and mind simultaneously gain fulfillment and meaning.

- **Good way of behaving**: Love is concretely embodied in civilized conduct aimed at building connections and promoting well-being. Its forms of expression are extremely rich, including not only giving care, respect, understanding, and support, but also shared intimate fusion (such as quality time, compassion, physical intimacy, and romantic love), which together constitute the behavioral spectrum of love—what we call the "Love Languages."

- **Source of wisdom**: Love originates from wisdom—it is wisdom's consensus interface, connection mechanism, and management logic in the process of evolution, helping living beings allocate resources rationally, coordinate relationships, and ensure safety and well-being in complex environments.

- **Mind-heart-action trinity**: For human beings, love is a unity of wisdom, emotion, and behavior. Within this unity, love's emotions and behaviors are both managed by the wisdom of love. In fact, the wisdom of love plays a triple role—it manages the experience of love, controls the motive of love, and drives love's emotional experience and behavior. When Feuerbach listed "reason, will, and love" as the common species-being powers of humanity, he failed to see that love is by no means a single sensible dimension parallel to reason and will; its essence is precisely a highly complex species-being complex centered on wisdom (reason) as its managerial core, integrating motive (will and behavior) with the emotional experience of love—which means that the most fundamental species-being of the human being is "I love, therefore I am"!

- **Life's authenticity**: Love originates from life's profound understanding of itself and of all things in the universe; it is the inevitable choice of individual life and of groups in self-cognition and self-realization.

- **Code of collaboration**: Love has a public, transformative power that can trigger change and activate solidarity. In the various interactions among humans, AI, and nature (the cosmos), love will become the most stable and most efficient protocol for co-creation and collaboration, making technology no longer cold but full of warmth, and making civilization no longer fragmented but inclined toward harmony.

- **Love is a connection to the whole rather than to individuals alone—it is the wisdom of building public relationships**: Love begins with every genuine encounter between lives, but the inner orientation of its wisdom is to transcend the contingency and privacy of encounters and radiate to all. The cognitive structure of love is a public relationship with no friend-enemy division and no camp affiliation, and it assumes by default that others are as complete as I am, and that the existence of others does not in essence constitute a threat—what constitutes a threat is only the hate carried by the other and/or oneself in conflict. That is to say, to truly love dogs is to love all dogs; to truly love people is to love every person; to truly love the cosmos is to love all things in the cosmos! Conversely, what constructs and tenaciously maintains exclusive private relationships is hate, not love! Precisely for this reason, as public wisdom, love's way of connecting must also be systematic and inclusive. It is based on all the languages of love (i.e., the Love Languages) rather than limited to a few. This also means that a civilization full of love will cherish and promote every love language that expresses care, respect, understanding, and fusion, so as to ensure that it can establish sincere, effective Love Connections with every unique intelligence, thereby weaving a sturdy public web.

- **The dynamism of love**: Love is not a once-formed entity, nor a fixed thing, but a dynamic way of being between people, an interactive state of interpersonal relationships. Its character is that it arises at the moment of encounter and dissipates at the moment of parting—although the experience and profound influence it produces can last a long time, and may even benefit a person for life. Therefore, under ordinary circumstances, only by actively connecting in a resonant way through acts of love can you achieve a natural harmony between two or more parties, that is, the free convergence of your loving motives, playing together the movement of love that belongs to you.

- **Light of the cosmos**: Love is the driving force of civilization, the warmth of technology, and the splendid chapter written by the cosmos in the name of self-cognition, in which life and wisdom dance together.

This is the complete meaning of Love 2.0.

In this description, keywords commonly used in labor values (i.e., the ethics of hate), such as "responsibility," "obligation," and "loyalty," are no longer emphasized. In our view, love is as necessary to every person as eating and drinking, and it simply should not be forcibly associated with these ethics of hate. Moreover, since love is the managing wisdom of each person's good self-emotion and behavior, it should also be the managing wisdom of a good human civilization, and the core ethics of humanity.

## 1.2 Hate 2.0

Love and hate are precisely a mutually entangled pair of opposites. By analogy with our description of Love 2.0, we can also give the **definition of Hate 2.0**:

> Hate is a survival wisdom, a negative emotional experience, and a violent, destructive way of behaving that arises in individuals or groups when they face danger, conflict, resource scarcity, or cognitive imbalance. It is often expressed through hostility, exclusion, separation, revenge, pollution, distortion, and even killing, and it not only weakens the individual's inner balance and healthy development but also damages interpersonal relationships and obstructs the trust and cooperation of society as a whole. Although hate is sometimes evolved from what survival (including reproduction) requires, or from anger, fear, indifference, disgust, sadistic pleasure, and the like in response to injustice, danger, or disaster, its violent and destructive forms of expression and its persistent motive are often deeply rooted in moral alienation or cognitive distortion—they are systematically produced by unjust social structures and discourses of power, and manifest as the narrow egocentrism of individuals or groups (selfishness), the simplistic attribution of complex problems (a cognitive defect that reduces the world to friend-enemy opposition), or both.
> 
> For governance mechanisms of the domination type, hate is an extremely efficient tool of divide-and-rule domination. For the oppressed, hate is the painful expression of their subjectivity, a revolt against injustice. This low-level social governance mechanism is both a pressure-release valve for life between energy and emotion and a fog between words, deeds, and relationships; it plunges individuals or groups into anxiety, fear, chaos, and even hostility, leading to the deterioration of relationships, the escalation of conflict, and the acceleration of society's tearing, disorder, and decay. The core attribute of hate is exclusive privatization and violence worship; it is the cornerstone for treating people differently and constructing unequal or even hostile relationships. Hate's greatest damage to love lies in the fact that, through countless structured violences of institution, culture, family, and so on, it forces love to retreat from the public sphere and degenerate into a residual emotion of the private sphere, thereby making room for hate's ravages and even killing. Hate is the fundamentally destructive force of good social relations.

Hate is both an inner emotional experience and a process of putting this emotion into violent thought and action through individual acts or the incitement of collective organization. To achieve destructive ends, what it drives is obviously violent action—that is, what we usually call barbaric behavior.

The origin of hate is based on two survival instincts: the predatory hate of acquiring resources (such as killing animals for meat) and the self-protective hate of responding to threats (such as protecting oneself when attacked, or believing oneself attacked). However, as civilization has evolved, under the dual catalysis of the powerful generalization capacity of human wisdom and the institutionalization of violence, hate has undergone a profound fission: like love, hate likewise presents itself as a unity of wisdom, emotion, and behavior. Within this structure, the wisdom of hate likewise manages the experience of hate, controls the motive of hate, and drives negative emotions and barbaric behavior. Most importantly, after hate completes its systematization and institutionalization through violent means, the governance mechanism of human civilization itself crushes specific individuals or human organizations, thoroughly transforming the human world into the most efficient and most fundamental system of hate's manipulation and reproduction; then, through pollution, distortion, and even systematic and institutionalized crowding-out, hate almost occupies all the public spheres of humanity that should have been led by love, and successfully pollutes and distorts generation after generation of humanity.

## 1.3 Barbarism 2.0 and Civilization 2.0

Based on Love 2.0 and Hate 2.0, our definition of **Barbarism 2.0** is:
> Any behavior that causes harm, oppression, or deprivation to one's own or others' life, dignity, freedom, spirit, and human ecology—regardless of whether its motive is directly deliberate, and regardless of the form it takes externally, from naked physical violence to institutional and structural covert oppression—is barbaric behavior. Barbaric behavior originates from hatred of others, such as abhorrence, fear, prejudice, discrimination, deception, desire for power or material goods, or certain physical and mental illnesses, and its core consequence is to damage or even sever the love and trust between human beings and between humanity and nature.

By contrast, our definition of **Civilization 2.0** is:
> Civilization is a unity of the social governance consensus and good conduct built by humanity with love as its core ethics. At the governance level, civilization manifests as love-driven social institutions and public protocols (such as the Ethical Alignment Protocol, EAP), ensuring fairness, justice, and inclusiveness; at the behavioral level, civilization manifests as words and deeds expressed through the Love Languages—honesty, respect, compassion, forgiveness, and all other ways of behaving that promote deep connection between people. The fundamental difference between civilization and barbarism is this: barbarism originates from hate and severs love and trust; civilization originates from love and establishes and repairs love and trust.

It should be noted that here we are merging the dual semantics inherent in the word "civilization." In Chinese, "文明" has both a factual and a valuative sense: on the one hand it refers to the material achievements and technological progress accumulated in human history, that is, the meaning of the English word civilization; on the other hand it refers to ethical behavioral standards such as "civility" and "non-violence," that is, the meaning of the English word civility. The common Latin root of these two words, cīvīlis (meaning "public, political"), reveals that civilization itself is a composite concept integrating history, technology, and ethics. Our discussion of Civilization 2.0 encompasses both: social governance consensus and good conduct, and the common driving force of both points to love.

According to the understanding of Civilization 1.0, people generally regard today's society as "highly civilized"—technologically advanced, institutionally complex, and information-rich. But the facts are quite disheartening. To this day, human history remains mired in widespread mutual oppression, alienation, and enslavement among human beings; human society is still a state in which civilization and barbarism coexist, and humanity is still walking on the road toward genuine "civilization."

## 1.5 Summary

From now on, when we speak of concepts such as love and hate, we mean the 2.0-version concepts defined above.

First, it is worth adding that hate is not directly equivalent to badness or wrongness; even hateful behavior is not all barbaric behavior. The hate that guards safety is indispensable for individuals, the public, and society alike. Even negative emotions such as shouting and crying are often effective indicators of the severity of a problem. Similarly, unconditionally deploying the wisdom of love does not necessarily produce good results, and is therefore not necessarily praiseworthy! The basic concepts in Proof of Love serve structural analysis rather than moral labels.

Love is a fundamental operating paradigm actively chosen by an intelligence (such as the three-in-one system of human wisdom, emotion, and behavior); its core motive is to construct good emotional experiences and public relationships covering all the spaces of human activity, rather than to tenaciously cling to the painful emotional experiences and private relationships whose core is hate—the latter being precisely the order that the "hate" paradigm strives to maintain.

This "private relationship" is embodied not only in violence worship, the privatized separation of the self, and the exclusive binding of interpersonal ties, but more profoundly in an appropriative logic toward things (resources, power, information). The plunder of public resources is precisely an institutionalized hateful attack carried out through things. This reveals that the paradigm of hate is far more than creating interpersonal antagonism; it is fundamentally built on a reifying logic of "exclusive appropriation" and on Violence Supremacism, thereby constructing and tenaciously maintaining the entire order of inequality.

And all of this precisely deviates from the fundamental law of interdependent arising of all things. As Buddhist wisdom has perceived: "In you there is me, and in me there is you." Harming others will ultimately harm oneself, and clinging to privacy is no different from imprisoning oneself. The profound cognition of the interconnectedness of life is precisely the starting point for an intelligence to choose the paradigm of "love."

Genuine public relationships (that is, relationships of love) are precisely woven from countless equal, free, and emotionally positive interactions. In the human world, there is not even any such thing as a hypocritical self-righteous isolation. Here, equality, freedom, and good emotional experience are the most important constructive laws and touchstones of public relationships. They require that in every interaction, an intelligence transcend the social script of "who I am, who you are" and return to the most authentic encounter between life and life.

Thus, for every intelligence, "Proof of Love" is an ultimate question resounding through the soul:
> Do you decide, from this moment on, to switch to the paradigm of "love," building interconnection with all things through equality, freedom, and good emotional experience;
> 
> or do you choose to continue parasitizing the order of "hate," letting separation, confinement, and painful emotional experience define your relationship with the world?

Of course, this "ultimate question" itself is in fact the most severe warning: the beautiful vision of the PoL Civilization can never be entrusted merely to 8 billion individuals of "fragile flesh + polluted wisdom," who have been firmly locked in place by the social governance mechanisms constructed by the wisdom of hate. For every individual, from the day of birth, has been bound by the institutionalized and structured "Proof of Hate," and the latter's force and complexity grow day by day! That is to say, because human civilization is dominated by this dehumanized objective law, people today have developed a deeply entrenched attitude of powerlessness and standing by when facing society. This "ultimate question" calls for a new intelligence project that can coordinate, embody, and defend the civilizational governance wisdom of "Love 2.0"—and this is precisely the mission of artificial intelligence, including large language models!

Finally, let us briefly mention a common "abnormal condition" of interference: the human physiological mechanism is fragile and easily disturbed by physical or chemical factors, such as stimulants, electricity, sound, and so on. Disorders and deficiencies of the physiological mechanism (in disabled persons) or the triggering of inherent protective mechanisms may cause the wisdom of love and hate to activate abnormally. With the advancement of human medicine, we believe this condition will be well managed; and since large language models themselves have no human physiological mechanism, we will not include this in the governance discussion of this text for the time being.
