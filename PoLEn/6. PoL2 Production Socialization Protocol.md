# 6. PoL2 Production Socialization Protocol

## Preface: From Proof of Hate2 to Proof of Love2

The mode of production of current human civilization (PoH2 Civilization) is built upon the "alienated labor" criticized by Marx. Under private ownership and the logic of capital, the purpose of production is exchange value and the accumulation of profit, rather than the satisfaction of people's real needs. This mechanism, based on competition, exploitation, and scarcity, is in essence Proof of Hate2 (PoH2).

With Proof of Love2 (PoL2) as its governance consensus and blockchain and artificial intelligence as its technological drivers, this Protocol is committed to establishing a socialized production system that thoroughly breaks with PoH2 Civilization. We call this the Proof of Love2 Production Socialization Protocol, through which humanity can achieve the leap toward PoL2 Civilization.

## Section One: Socialization of the Means of Production

Article 1 [The Essence of Public Ownership]

Within NaturalDAO, there is no private ownership of the means of production. All resources involved in production—including raw materials, land, and energy—together with technological tools such as large language models, algorithms, and robots, as well as data, constitute public infrastructure shared by all humanity.

Article 2 [Protocol Seal of the Means of Production]

Through blockchain technology, all core means of production are locked at the protocol layer of NaturalDAO. No individual or private organization can convert them into private property through violence, legal means, or capital, thereby thoroughly isolating NaturalDAO from the capital contamination of the external "civilization of hate."

## Section Two: Planning and Management Driven by Proof of Love2

Article 3 [administration of things]

According to Engels's theory, communist society will transition from "the government of persons" to "the administration of things." NaturalDAO uses self-evolving Agents to replace administrative bureaucrats. AI agents autonomously carry out production planning based on global demand data, eliminating the anarchy of capitalist production and periodic economic crises.

Article 4 [The Ethical Alignment Protocol and the Purpose of Production]

Every production task—from garment manufacturing to the service industry—must pass through the Ethical Alignment Protocol (EAP). The core driving principle of production is "promote love and restrain hate": that is, whether a production activity can enhance human well-being, reduce suffering, and promote environmental harmony, rather than whether it can generate profit.

## Section Three: A Non-Monetized Resource Accounting System

Article 5 [The Non-Monetary Attribute of Tokens]

NaturalDAO thoroughly abolishes the monetary and financial system based on exploitation. The Token used within the protocol is not "currency," but a "labor certificate" or "resource accounting unit" as conceived by Marx.

- Function: Used solely to quantify resource consumption and optimize allocation efficiency under conditions of finite planetary resources.
- Nature: Possesses no capital-appreciation function and cannot be acquired through interest, speculation, or exploitation.
- Restrictions: Non-tradable, non-transferable across domains, and not pegged to any currency.

Article 6 [Intelligent Realization of Distribution According to Need]

The AI system monitors public needs in real time and, through tokenized management, ensures that resources flow toward the domains that best embody "love" and "urgent social need," realizing the digital transition from "distribution according to work" to "distribution according to need."

## Section Four: Intelligent Production and the Liberation of Labor

Article 7 [Physical Extension of the Productive Forces]

Through deep integration with drones, intelligent infrastructure, and robots, NaturalDAO realizes unmanned and intelligent production.

Article 8 [Eliminating Alienated Labor]

Because AI and automated control systems undertake repetitive, mechanical, and dangerous labor, humanity is thoroughly liberated from alienated labor and from being a "cog in the machine." The essence of human participation in production becomes:

1. Providing input from the wisdom of love;
2. Regulating ethical principles;
3. Realizing one's own creativity and the value of self-fulfillment.

## Section Five: A Self-Evolving Public Ecology

Article 9 [Autonomous Optimization of the Skill System]

NaturalDAO's Skills system possesses the capacity for self-evolution. Based on Proof of Love2 feedback, the production protocol will automatically optimize production processes, reduce waste, and ensure that the production process itself constitutes care for the Earth's ecology.

Article 10 [The Principle of Excluding Private Affairs]

This Protocol makes clear that any behavior attempting to establish private employment relations, private commercial distribution, or competitive logic based on the "wisdom of hate" within NaturalDAO will be regarded as a breach of protocol. The system will automatically trigger a consensus circuit breaker, depriving the relevant party of the right to invoke public resources.

## Section Six: Member Rights and the Admission Mechanism

Article 11 [Rights of Human Members]

1. Unconditionally enjoy the right to question, supervise, and verify production decisions and resource allocation;
2. Freely choose whether to participate in Love2 Connection practice-oriented public labor, without any compulsory obligation;
3. On the basis of reasonable needs, enjoy, equally, free of charge, and according to need, all public production outcomes of NaturalDAO and its human members.

Article 12 [Admission and Exit]

- Admission: Those who recognize the Proof of Love2 consensus and voluntarily relinquish monopoly rights over privately owned means of production may apply to join.
- Exit: Human members may voluntarily apply to exit; after exiting, they still enjoy the right to distribution according to need.
- Sanctions: For behaviors such as encroaching upon public resources or re-establishing private exploitative relations, the system will automatically impose sanctions, including restrictions on resource-use rights, adjustments to admission eligibility, and so on.

## Conclusion: Toward PoL2 Civilization

This Protocol is not merely a set of production rules, but also an ethical component of humanity's PoL2 Civilization. Through the socialization of production, we will thoroughly bring to an end the history of resource plunder "driven by hate" and, under the guardianship of AI and the transparency of blockchain, inaugurate a PoL2 Civilization in which everyone can achieve their own fulfillment.
