\section*{Positionality and competing interests}
\label{sec:statements}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Positionality and competing interests}
The author is one of the contributors listed in the \pol{} text and contributes to the NaturalDAO governance workstream whose pilot protocol this analysis discusses.
The author has a reputational interest in that work and in the framework.
Several recommendations in \cref{sec:design} coincide with choices already made in that pilot protocol, and each such coincidence is flagged so that readers can judge whether the evidence or the prior design drove it.
To limit the effect of this position, the analysis uses a codebook that is symmetric between supporting and negative findings, appraises every coded study, reports certainty for each headline finding, offers at least one text-preserving option for every tension, lists the corrections to its earlier dataset release, and releases all coding with rationales.
The tension analysis was not reviewed by the framework's originator or by other contributors before release.
The author has no financial relationship with TypeSafe AI or with the developers of any model discussed.

\section*{Use of AI assistance}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Use of AI assistance}
We report the use of AI tools as arXiv's policy on generative AI requires. AI agents (Claude Opus 5.5, Anthropic), which are not authors, worked under the author's direction: they ran the searches, screened records, extracted data from full texts with locators, coded and appraised the studies, served as the blind second coder, wrote the figure scripts and drafted text, which the author reviewed and revised over successive rounds. Fresh agents of the same model checked the numbers in the text against their sources in separate review passes.
The present version was restructured and edited for clarity with the assistance of Claude Sonnet 5.5 (Anthropic); the author reviewed the edited text, and scripted checks compared its numbers and citations with the previous version and the data.
Every reference was generated from, or checked against, a registry record: corpus entries from arXiv and Zenodo metadata, background entries against Crossref, arXiv or OpenAlex, and grey sources against their source pages.
The agents' instructions, findings and resolutions are released with the data.
The author set the research question, the requirements, the codebook and the decision rules, directed the work, and takes full responsibility for all content.
The author did not re-code or re-check individual study codes by hand, so the coding is AI-generated and its agreement statistics compare two AI coders (\cref{sec:method}).
