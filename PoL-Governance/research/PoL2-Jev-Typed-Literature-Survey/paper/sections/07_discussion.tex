\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Beyond one specification.}
Any proposal that puts an automated filter in front of AI systems faces G1--G5 and, where it automates oversight, the escalation question of G6.
Constitutional classifiers, the Chinese duty to stop unlawful generation and the DSA's provisions on automated means all move toward such a layer~\cite{sharma2025constitutional,cac2023genai,dsa2022}.
G7 and the rest of G6 arise only where a proposal also gives disputes to AI or scores people.
The case also shows that a governance text can be read clause by clause as a set of testable requirements, and that this reading exposes tensions that a reading of the text as a statement of values does not.

\paragraph{Certainty.}
No finding reaches moderate certainty, and four are contested (\cref{tab:keyfindings}): that injection rarely selects the attacker's target, that escalation to a stronger reasoner saves cost without loss of accuracy, and the two threshold findings.
Each is opposed by at least one study.
For the two threshold findings the opposing preregistered study ranks as high as the strongest supporting one, so both fall from moderate to low (\oa{C}).
The rating rules were revised during internal review, after reviewers found that they worked only upwards, and every finding was then re-rated on the whole corpus (\oa{B}).
All ratings are low or very low because the sources are preprints posted within days of a model's release, many results rest on one study, the one reproduction of a headline study re-ran its authors' code and data, and no study measures the specification's own construct.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
The corpus covers 24 days of unrefereed preprints and grey literature, many from single authors working quickly.
Of the 27 early studies graded by a community tracker, none was rated at low risk of bias~\cite{rafe2026awesome}, and studies of failures may be over-represented.
Several studies use open models rather than the hosted one, and some do not report the hosted version.
The tallies pool hosted and open models.
The requirements are derived from \pol{}, so the analysis may miss strengths of typed models that it does not ask about.
Screening, coding and verification were carried out by AI agents of one model family under the author's direction, so the agreement statistic ($\kappa = \nKappaAll{}$) is an upper bound on how reproducible the codebook is for human coders. It also implies that a share of the pairs coded only once would be coded differently on a second reading.
Eighteen of the 103 coded studies dated by 1~October entered the corpus only after the run of 8~October: 5 arXiv papers not retrieved on 1 or 4~October, possibly because the search interface changed, and 13 Zenodo records whose earlier screening outcome is unknown. The search may therefore still have missed some.
No study compares a typed model with trained human reviewers on a governance task, so we cannot say whether its errors are worse than those of the people it would assist.

\paragraph{What would change the conclusion.}
We would revise ``not a judge'' if, on a held-out, independently annotated three-valued \pol{} benchmark in Chinese, a typed model with risk-controlled thresholds kept its false-block and miss rates within targets set in advance, changed fewer answers under name and definition swaps than twice its repeat-noise floor, flipped fewer than 5\% of decisions under single-opinion insertion and a 64-query adaptive attack, and agreed with an independent panel of trained reviewers at least as well as the reviewers agree with each other, in one preregistered study with an independent replication or in two independent studies.
If generative evaluators tested under the same protocol passed where typed models failed, the conclusion would become specific to typed models. Until then it is not.
The first open problem is therefore that benchmark.
It needs independent annotation, disagreement-aware labels~\cite{davani2022dealing}, time stamps and periodic refreshing~\cite{dibonaventura2025hatevolution}, and a held-out set kept outside any repository.
Other open problems are a comparison of typed detectors with trained human reviewers on the same governance items, generative comparators for the failures that were never compared, tests of whether hiding or coarsening probabilities and restricting queries reduce attacks, and independent replication of the main findings on new data.
