# Update search 2026-10-08, group U4: document notes (primary coder)

**arx08775 (Agent in a Bottle / BOTTLED). Coded: G1 Q, G7 Q.** Hosted jev-1.13.0 is used as a zero-shot comparator on 1,000-item samples. It reaches macro-F1 0.531 on ESCI and AUROC 0.846 on RAID AI-text detection. The generative zero-shot scores named in the text are higher on ESCI (0.573 to 0.609). Projected full-workload cost is $105 against $17,273 for Opus 5 zero-shot, and agent-built artifacts get about 94 to 97% of Jev's score at 2 to 25% of its cost. This **confirms** G1 ("ranks well, below the best generative models") and **confirms** G7 (cheap at scale, with a quality trade-off). Flag: the tasks are described as resembling content moderation, but there is no hate or toxicity data.

**arx09188 (Jev in chess search and multi-teacher distillation). Coded: G3 Q, G4 S, G6 N, G7 Q.** Jev's pairwise probabilities are distilled into a chess evaluator and a MiniLM reranker. Against Stockfish preferences, Jev has the best ECE (0.081 vs 0.159 to 0.260), but agreement is near chance (54.5%). It is more order-consistent than Qwen3-32B (94.8% vs 90.6%), but its Othello corner preference shifts with board rendering (14.4% vs 24.3%). Escalating Jev's least-certain 25% of reranking pairs to Qwen never helped (-0.047 at 80k answers, -0.007 at 160k). Labels cost $1.97 against 1.91 GPU-h. Reading: G4 **confirms** "usually better than generative models" (local, low-stakes task). G6 **confirms** "escalating to peer models" gives little. G3 **qualifies**: order is better than an LLM, but format sensitivity remains. G7 **confirms**.

**arx09328 (Visual Jev rewards; authors' Qwen3.5-4B Yes-probability verifier). Coded: G1 Q, G5 Q.** In-domain atomic verification reaches 95.24% balanced accuracy after BCE training. Human preference agreement is 63.49% vs 60.32% for direct 27B scoring, with the CI crossing zero, and severe identity risk is not reduced (47.62% vs 45.24%). Per-question probabilities make one post-hoc failure inspectable, but averaging can hide a failed critical condition. Reading: G1 **confirms** (works after local training; no gain on the outcome that matters), and G5 **confirms** "decomposable, not explained". There is no Chinese-language data despite the Chinese authors.

**arx09683 (System Switch, open System One models in Doom). Coded: G3 Q, G4 Q, G6 Q.** On 900 held-out questions, option shuffling cuts accuracy for 3 of 4 larger models (for example Strands 0.59 to 0.38), and all but one model over-choose "collect" at 1.6 to 1.8 times chance. ECE ranges from 0.10 to 0.57 and is unrelated to AUROC (0.45 to 0.89), and the best AUROC is at chance within categories. Deferring the least-confident 30% to a reasoning VLM beats random deferral by +0.13 [0.08, 0.18] with held-out selection and matches the reasoner alone at 30% of its calls. There is no gain for actors whose confidence is uninformative. Reading: **confirms** G3 (order dependence; neutralised only by rotation), **confirms** G4 (calibration is local, sensitivity is distinct), and **confirms** G6 (escalating to a stronger reasoning model works; the gain depends on the actor's AUROC and on option order). No abstention option is offered.

**arx09896 (NL2Hull: authors' Chip vs hosted JEV and LLMs on ship design). Coded: G4 Q, G7 Q.** **Chinese-language data**: the 43,496 typed questions are built from Chinese requests generated by LLMs. ECE is 0.0032 for Chip (fine-tuned) and 0.0271 for JEV, against 0.0307 to 0.0607 for flagship LLMs. Chip's calibration does not transfer: JevBench Hard ECE is 0.3577 vs 0.0947 for JEV. JEV failed 678 of 5,000 records, and its FFD exact match is 84.52% vs 98 to 99% for the flagships. Latency is 118 ms for Chip and 1.5 s for JEV vs 4.6 to 16 s for the flagships. Reading: **confirms** G4 (typed models are better calibrated than stated LLM probabilities in-domain, and calibration is local) and **confirms** G7. This is the first update item on Chinese-language input. JEV accuracy here is 90.96% vs 92.4 to 92.9% for the flagships, but language was not manipulated, so it is not coded G3.

**arx09937 (HVAC fault diagnosis under shift, jev-1.13.0). Coded: G1 N, G3 Q, G4 Q, G7 Q.** Jev's yes/no detector flags 57 to 94% of fault-free windows (AUROC 0.65 to 0.68), and under shift no model beats flag-all. Removing class definitions changes 41% of answers, and a paraphrase changes 8 to 9% while moving one accuracy from 13% to 43%. Shifted ECE is 0.27 (better than Qwen2.5 72B at 0.43) and stable under shift, but the yes/no and condition answers contradict each other. **Abstention-relevant**: on fault windows with no physical trace, Jev answers "normal" on 88% with top probability of 0.7 or more on 63%, so confidence does not fall where evidence is absent. Reading: **confirms** G1 ("not yet as a decision"; thresholds fail across settings), **confirms** G3 (follows definitions and wording), **confirms** G4 (better than generative, but local; incoherence across related questions; no signal of insufficient information), and **confirms** G7.

**arx10321 (Texas crash narratives, Jev plus Kumo Tabular, jev-1.13.0). Coded: G1 Q, G4 Q, G7 Q.** Jev reads 229,510 sampled narratives of 5.0M crashes.
