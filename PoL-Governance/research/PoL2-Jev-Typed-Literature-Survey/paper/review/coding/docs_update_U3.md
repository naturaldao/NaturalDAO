## arx07177: CLM-as-a-Judge (Nandakishore)
- **Tier: coded** (G1 N, G3 Q, G4 Q, G6 N). Open, author-independent, preregistered study of an open contrastive typed model (CLM-v0.1-8B, with laya as a baseline) as a judge on RewardBench, RewardBench 2, RM-Bench, JudgeBench and HaluEval, against Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-32B and a reward model.
- Key numbers: accuracy 0.351-0.593, at chance on RM-Bench and JudgeBench; one constant label on HaluEval (0.581). Order flips 0.0002 against 0.2188 for the generative judge, but laya flips 0.4395. Raw overconfidence +0.235 to +0.401, repaired by one fitted temperature to ECE <= 0.062. The cascade escalates 0.923-1.000 of items.
- Answer boxes: confirms G1 (a weak typed model is worse than a same-size generative judge). G3: CLM's structural order invariance qualifies the box in its favour, while laya's flips confirm it. Confirms G4 (calibration is repairable only with fitted local maps). Confirms G6 (a gate adds nothing when the first tier is near chance, and thresholds do not transfer).

## arx07327: SharedKV-BT (Wake, Wagle; Microsoft)
- **Tier: coded** (G3 N, G4 N, G5 Q, G6 Q). Authors' Jev-style typed readout on Qwen2.5-3B inside behaviour trees for robot and computer-use agents.
- Key numbers: candidate reordering changed 46/120 node-local decisions. **Abstention:** with an explicit ABSTAIN option, 28/30 invalid cases were rejected but only 17/30 valid ones were answered correctly (45/60 against 51/60 for autoregressive generation). Node-local decomposition raised joint decisions from 90 to 113/120. The model's own completion claim would have ended the task early in 10/15 replayed states.
- Answer boxes: confirms G3 (order dependence) and G4 (an explicit abstain option does not work as designed). Confirms G5 (separate stage-local steps make chains inspectable and more accurate). Confirms G6 (an independently built rule must hold authority).

## arx07716: Readout stability in prefill-only decision models (Li, Chen; HKUST)
- **Tier: coded** (G3 N, G4 Q, G6 Q). Seven open typed families on 16 datasets: whether menu-only changes are predictable from the first pass (they are, within 4.2 points, unlike generative models), and how to spend inference compute.
- Key numbers: question wording alone moved Open-Jev-2B from 0.454 to 0.714, and its direction reverses across families. Correct-label-first on a small menu: 0.622 to 0.426. Permutation averaging cut ECE from 0.09 to 0.03 without accuracy gain. A kev-0.8B to 4B cascade beats re-asking at matched cost (0.684 at 1.28 units vs 0.668 at 1.44). The decider gate did not beat random on every source.
- Answer boxes: confirms G3 (wording and order effects; rotation/averaging mitigates) and G4. Confirms G6 (escalation to a stronger model helps, re-asking the same model does not; gates need per-model validation). **Emotion:** includes GoEmotions/CARER emotion label spaces, where the restriction predictor has a fixed +2.5 to +4.9 point residual; no emotion-recognition accuracy result relevant to T1 is reported.

## arx07730: SanSi, a looped typed decision model (Gan, Lee, Kang; Minnesota)
- **Tier: coded** (G4 Q). Authors' looped open typed model (Ouro backbone) on 10,027 English decisions, with Jev API (jev-1.13.0) as an untrained-on-our-data reference.
- Key numbers: **abstention / missing evidence:** SanSi's evidence AUROC was 0.935 and it gave a hard answer to 17.5% of 382 unanswerable items, while Jev API gave a hard answer to 62.8% of them. Confidence rises on wrong answers too (0.567 to 0.660), and 19.7% of errors have confidence >= 0.9. ECE was 0.093 (Jev 0.066), falling to 0.022-0.029 only with a fitted temperature. On liar chains Jev API fell from 100% (1 step) to chance from 6 steps (App. G.6). This is a capability result and is not coded.
- Answer box: confirms G4. Missing evidence is often answered confidently by hosted Jev, confident errors would pass a 0.9 gate, and calibration needs a labelled fit.

## arx07953: Benchmarking System One Models in Online Moderation (Mazzoni, Failla)
- **Tier: coded** (G1 Q, G3 Q, G4 Q, G6 Q). **Hate speech / content moderation, central to the safety valve and hate-language clauses.** Hosted Jev v1.13 and open Laya v0.3 on PluRule (Reddit community rules), HateModerate (Facebook hate-speech policy, 41 categories), OLID, HateXplain and ModerationBench (Bluesky). Conditions vary written rules, retrieved precedents and answer space. **English only; no Chinese data.**
- Key numbers: Jev's F1 is competitive with Detoxify (66.1 vs 65.7 on OLID, 65.5 vs <= 57.0 on HateXplain), but its OLID accuracy is lower (74.4 vs 81.0). ModerationBench AUROC was 0.893-0.940. Exact hate-speech policy selection was 29.4% without precedents and 51.6% with them. On PluRule test, Jev without precedents (48.4%) was below always-'no violation' (50.0) and published GPT-5.2 (54.1-57.7). Laya flagged everything (100% recall, 6.2% exact match). Semantic vs opaque policy names cost Jev 5.06 points of binary accuracy. Noul ECE was 14.9-33.2% without precedents. At confidence >= 0.8, coverage was 71.9% at 94.3% accuracy on ModerationBench, but accepted accuracy was only 67.6% on PluRule test.
- Answer boxes: confirms G1 ('a ranking, not yet a decision': good AUROC, weak operating points, rule-cluster differences hidden by aggregates; App. C). Confirms G3 (label names shift the operating point). Confirms G4 (not consistently calibrated; precedents help). Confirms G6: the 0.8 gate works on some sets but leaves a quarter to a third of accepted hate-policy and community-rule decisions wrong. No direction change. Laya's results reinforce that robustness varies by model.

## arx08089: When Plans Change Answers (Kim; KAIST)
- **Tier: non-empirical (screening tier confirmed).** This is a formal semantic-query-optimisation paper whose object is calibrated typed decisions (Jev Noul in JEVDB) with escalation bands. The simulations use a synthetic workload in which calibration holds by construction, and no typed model is measured. Real-engine evaluation is explicitly left for future work.
- Rows recorded with empirical = no (G5 Q, G6 Q), not tallied: operator-level guarantees do not carry over to query outputs (recall 0.88 to 0.58 in the worked example). Bands calibrated on a plan's own candidates make pushdown unsound. The argument is consistent with the G5 and G6 boxes (chains compose errors; thresholds fixed on one population miss targets on another) and changes neither.

## arx08155: S1-MAS, System One-guided multi-agent collaboration (Zhou et al.; UESTC)
- **Tier: context.** Open Laya selects inspection targets, next tasks and termination for GPT-4o workers on MMLU, GSM8K, AQuA, MultiArith, SVAMP, HumanEval and WebSRC. It reached 93.97% mean accuracy with 78.3-94.0% fewer GPT tokens than five MAS baselines. In the ablation, the Laya controller scored 84.17% against 83.14% for GPT-4o-mini routing (75.5% fewer tokens) and 82.34% for random routing.
- Not coded: the typed model makes engineering coordination choices on benchmark reasoning. It does not detect anything or gate on confidence, the study has no oversight design, and it makes no claim about public-scale deployment. There are no intervals, only SDs over three seeds. It bears on no G1-G7 answer box.

## arx08675: Same-Number Citation Swaps, Jev as a financial evidence judge (Xu et al.)
- **Tier: coded** (G1 Q, G3 Q, G6 Q). Hosted Jev (jev-1.13-20260917) as a Noul source-support verifier for GPT-4.1-mini financial calculation traces (FinQA), stress-tested with same-number citation swaps, equivalent-fact controls and an explicit-column rendering, plus a fresh 36-page bank with a blinded non-author reviewer.
- Key numbers: AUROC 0.986 [0.964, 0.999] on natural traces, but a zero-call signed-number rule nearly matched it (141 vs 142 joint passes). 4 of 6 wrong-role swaps were accepted at 0.8. Explicit headers flipped all 8 wrong-role calls to withheld, but also lowered support for valid equivalents. The fresh bank withheld 11/19 equivalent alternatives. The prospectively specified < 0.8 consult gate had nothing to correct (95/96 already correct; the single error sat at exactly 0.8), and fallback changed matches by -1.56 pp [-4.17, 0].
- Answer boxes: confirms G1 (high aggregate discrimination, errors in sub-types the aggregate hides). Confirms G3 (sensitivity to the rendering of identical evidence). Confirms G6 (confident errors can pass a fixed gate, and escalation to a peer generative trace added nothing). **Abstention:** withholding is the verifier's only abstain mechanism, and it removes valid evidence along with wrong roles.
