# NaturalDAO BD Outreach Tracker

This tracker follows the 30 prospects in [NaturalDAO_BD_Contact_Map.md](NaturalDAO_BD_Contact_Map.md). The names, roles, contact routes, outreach angles, and sources are carried over from that map.

## How to use

- Keep one row per person. Update the contact and reply fields after each interaction.
- All rows start as **待联系** / **未联系**. The repository's outreach email documents are drafts and do not establish that any email was sent.
- Record the actual first and most recent contact dates, channel, reply summary, next action, and follow-up date. Do not infer contact or reply status.
- Keep the contact map as the source for target fit and research context; use this CSV as the operational log.

## Suggested status values

- 联系状态：待联系、已联系待回复、已回复、跟进中、已安排沟通、已达成合作、暂不合作、联系方式无效
- 回复状态：未联系、未回复、已回复、已转介、婉拒、自动回复
